FIELD BINOCULARS

A replica of a pair of 6 x 30 German field binoculars, printed after
returning from an Immersion experienced with Sarah Hall in
Kebechet.

This basic design was fielded by the Germans in the Second World
War, and was in production, based on a civilian design, from 1907
until 1940.

Analysis: Kane, R

The date of manufacture stamped on those is 1915. That would
suggest that rather than being 'standard issue', these may well
have been a pair that saw action in the World War of 1914-1918,
and had been given to a son when he joined the army in the late
'30s or early '40s.
