PIECE OF BROKEN STATUE

This is a fragment of an obelisk that once towered outside the
Government Building in Cairo, but was destroyed during an
engagement with an NRC Autonomous Sentry Platform (ASP).

Originally topped with a statue of Horus in falcon form and
adorned with hieroglyphs copied from inscriptions taken from the
Valley of the Kings, the obelisk was meant to represent the rebirth
and endurance of Egypt, connecting its past to its present to its
future.
