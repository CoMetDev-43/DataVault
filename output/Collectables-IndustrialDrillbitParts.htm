INDUSTRIAL DRILLBIT PARTS

These are parts of the drill head assembly on one of the Mobile
Water Refineries used in the construction of the ill-fated Sub-Cairo
Aquifers project that was intended to provide much needed water
to the parched population of Cairo.

The Mobile Water Refineries were part mobile drilling platform,
part water reclamation system; designed to switch roles at
different parts of the project cycle, first drilling down into the vast
sandstone aquifers below Cairo, before enlarging them and
linking the huge porous vaults together, then drawing water from
them, purifying and sanitizing it for Human consumption.

The drill bit heads are built from synthetic diamond, Carbon 's
hardest naturally occurring form, and were manufactured on the
entirely self-sufficient lumbering platforms.
