BREAKING NEWS

06/02/60 - 08:20 - SINGAPORE

Unconfirmed reports are coming in of an explosion on the North bank of the
Singapore Marina, in one of the facilities making up the Underground
Science City.

At this time the cause of the explosion is unknown, but large plumes of dark
smoke have been seen rising from the site.

City Authorities urge the public not to panic, and to not hinder the
emergency teams that are converging on the area.

UPDATE: 06/02/60 - 08:30 - SINGAPORE

Sources on the ground are confirming that the explosion took place at the
Singapore Research Laboratories belonging to the Coalescence Corporation,
a multinational Medical Research company with substantial interests in the
fields of Robotics and Defense.

The Coalescence Corporation has issued the following statement: "At this
difficult and uncertain time, our thoughts and prayers are with the families
and friends of those who work at our Singapore Research Centre."

UPDATE: 06/02/60 - 08:34 - SINGAPORE

Singapore Military Authorities have implemented a no-fly order in response
to the apparent crash of a several scrambled relief VTOLs that were
heading to the scene of the explosion.

A large scale mobilization of the Singapore Armed Forces has begun, with
reports starting to emerge of roadblocks being established on major
thoroughfares, turning back traffic from either approaching or departing
from the scene of the apparent accident.

UPDATE: 06/02/60 - 08:45 - SINGAPORE

Singapore Military Authorities have officially announced a state of
emergency and declared martial law; they are enacting procedures put in
place in case of the event of a terrorist biological or chemical attack.

Drones are over flying an area of Singapore approximately 30 square miles
in size, broadcasting repeating messages advising people to stay in their
homes.

UPDATE: 06/02/60 - 09:30 - SINGAPORE

Military drones have been seen flocking around, and in some cases shooting
down civilian drones violating the no-fly order.

Pictures uploaded to the internet from one of these drones before its
destruction show the Coalescence Corporation complex, where dense
clouds of smoke continue to billow into the air above the city; numerous
bodies have been seen in the grounds of the building and in the surrounding
city streets. Nothing appears to be moving.

Shots have apparently been fired at some of the newly established blockade
points to the West of Hill Top Park and to the South West of Chinatown, as
panicking citizens attempt to flee this newly declared "Quarantine Zone".

UPDATE: 06/02/60 - 10:20 - SINGAPORE

Riot Police have dispersed an angry mob to the South West of China Town
using knock out gas and LRAD sonic area control weapons.

A large convoy of Military Trucks carrying Hazmat suited individuals has
been waved through the barricade and is making its way towards the
downtown area.

UPDATE: 06/02/60 - 10:45 - SINGAPORE

Cargo Drones are crisscrossing the sky; scooping up water from the Marina
Bay before dumping it in plumes over the city, where smoke continues to rise
into the sky.

We are being told that James Chung, the commander of the Singapore
Armed Forces will be holding a briefing at 11:30 local time. We will bring you
live updates as it proceeds. The page will automatically refresh.

LIVE UPDATES: EVENT HAS CONCLUDED 06/02/60 - 11:30 - SINGAPORE

James Chung, commander of the Singapore Armed Forces has issued a
short statement about the developing situation on the ground. Points follow.

· At 08:17 this morning, an explosion occurred at the Coalescence
Corporation's research facility, situated at Science City near the Marina.
Fires are still burning out of control.

· At this time, the cause of the explosion is unknown. While terrorism hasn't
been ruled out, there have been no claims of responsibility from any
organization.

· Contact was lost with 2 military VTOLs scrambled to the site. The VTOLs
then crashed.

· Early reports from the ground suggest a 'substantial loss of life', both in the
building and in the surrounding area.

· A 30 mile quarantine zone has been established to contain the area, until
more is understood about the nature of the threat to public health.

· Citizens living in the area are being asked to stay in their homes, and to
keep their doors and windows closed.

· A state of Martial Law has been declared, and will remain in place until the
nature of the incident is more firmly understood.
