Masud Assaf

My Unit 03/02/63

Mother you would be happy today.

I have made some good friends at my new post.

This is all because of the amazing Ghorayeba care packages that you sent
last week. The entire unit devoured them in ten minutes.

Two of my new friends, Naeem and Ottah, are from the city. Another, Yazid,
is from a small town in the north. I've never seen a man eat as much as he
does.

I will write more soon.
