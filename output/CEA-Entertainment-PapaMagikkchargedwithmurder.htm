PAPA MAGIKK CHARGED WITH MURDER

Former hip-hop music mogul Dre "Papa Magikk" Miller was indicted by a
Dade County grand jury late Monday evening on a first-degree murder
charge for his alleged involvement in a shooting incident in July that left three
men dead.

The incident allegedly began shortly after Miller and fellow musician Jim
"Baby Duck" Mackey arrived at a Miami night club. Within minutes of their
arrival a scuffle ensued between Miller and Mackey's associates, witnesses
inside the club reported.

"All I saw was a bunch of guys punching each other, then "bang, bang,
bang," a machine gun or something popped off," said Jaylee Smith, 23, of
Miami who was with her friends for a night out."

Reports that at least six of Mackey's associates left the club in emergency
medical vehicles were confirmed by the Dade County Sheriff's department
Sunday afternoon.

"Three of Mr. Mackey's associates were pronounced dead on the scene by
county emergency," said Dade County Sheriff's department spokeswoman,
Lisa Suarez.

Miller is currently being held without bond in the Dade County Jail pending
arraignment.
