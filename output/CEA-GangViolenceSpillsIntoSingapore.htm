BREAKING NEWS

54 IMMORTALS VIOLENCE SPILLS OUT OF QUARANTINE ZONE

SINGAPORE

The 54 Immortals (54I), the Militarized Criminal Combine that has been the
ruling power in the Singapore Quarantine Zone for the last 3 years, has been
involved in running battles through the Quarantine Zone that have spilled out
into the main body of Singapore, with assailants or forces unknown.

Eye witnesses report fires burning out of control, along with large scale
looting carried out by members of the 54I. The criminal forces are reported
to have been accompanied by aerial drone support.

Unsubstantiated rumors suggest that the Goh siblings, the 54I 's brutal
leaders have been killed in the conflict.

If true, what this means for the 54I remains unclear - however aerial footage
shows ongoing running battles between 54I forces within the Quarantine
Zone, suggesting that there may have been no clearly established line of
succession. More will certainly become clear in the coming days and weeks.

The Singapore Armed Forces have welcomed the news, but other than
denying their involvement in the incident, have declined any further
comment at this time.
