INTERNATIONAL AID TO QUARANTINE ZONE SUSPENDED

09/23/61 - 20:02 - SINGAPORE

The United Nations announced today that all international aid efforts to the
Singapore Quarantine Zone would be suspended, effective immediately.

Citing evidence that aid being air-lifted into the area was falling into the
hands of criminals operating inside of the quarantine walls and was being
sold for profit, the head of the aid efforts said that they had no choice but to
terminate the program. They will also be pulling medical and science
workers out, as the Singapore Defense Forces are unable to guarantee their
safety.

The UN has called on the Singapore Authorities to do more to remove the 54
Immortals from within the Quarantine Zone. The Singapore Authorities
meanwhile have stated that due to budgetary constraints they are unable to
field an effective counter-force to the well-trained and well-equipped
criminals who are now apparently operating within the Quarantine Zone with
impunity.

What this means for the fate of the 100,000 or so remaining residents of the

Quarantine Zone isn't immediately obvious.

The UN describe the situation as 'an unprecedented humanitarian
catastrophe atop a humanitarian catastrophe'.
