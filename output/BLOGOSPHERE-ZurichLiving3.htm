Trip to Nice, France

So even though I wrecked my car two weeks ago at the mall. Daddy said I
deserved a break from all the stress of the repairs and paid for my shopping
adventure to Nice! Thank you Daddy! #loveyoulots

The jet was so uncomfortable, we couldn't recline the seats because of
some weird reason so we had to sit there with the seat backs up for the
whole flight. #frustrating!

Tara and I loved the couture shops. We even visited the Dravinski one right
on Avenue de la Californie. It was wonderfully decadent. The store changes
theme each week, so we got to see this amazing set of thirty cyclops
mannequins all with lasers shooting out of their eyes. It felt like we were at an
EDM RAVE or something.

Overall, it was a fun trip until Tara ate too much caviar and barfed in the
hotel suite. She was pretty sick on Sunday so I only got to go shopping for
four hours. Oh well.

Maybe next month we can come back.
