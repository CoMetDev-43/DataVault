CIA

Formed in 1947, the Central Intelligence Agency ( CIA ) is a critical
wing of the United States intelligence gathering community. The CIA
was born out of the Office of Strategic Services (OSS), an
organization that coordinated a myriad of covert activities against
the Axis Powers during World War II.

Operating in over 307 embassies and consulates around the world,
the CIA's primary mission is to collect, analyze and distribute
critical intelligence to the United States government
policymakers. The CIA does not engage in foreign policy decisions
related to national security and operates as an independent outlet
to aid those who do. Upon the request of the President of the
United States the CIA may carry out or oversee covert operations
activities involving its own operatives, specific members of the
United States Military or its allies.

Mission

The CIA has a number of distinct activities in which they operate.
These activities include but are not limited to the following:
intelligence gathering and the analysis of that data,
Counterintelligence on priority targets, Cyber intelligence to
engage or deter electronic threats to the United States and
Counterterrorism to suppress and prevent terrorist activities
against the United States or its allies.

Framework

The CIA has a diverse set of objectives and as such, is broken down
into the following organizational structure to help support those
needs.

· Directorate of Intelligence functions as the primary source for data

analysis and investigation related to international interests.

· Directorate of Support serves as the administrative unit that provides key

logistical support for CIA operations.

· Directorate of Science and Technology is critical for developing,

researching and creating technology that can be utilized by the United
States intelligence apparatus.

· National Clandestine Service primary purpose is to coordinate and collect

foreign intelligence from covert operations across the U.S. intelligence
organization.

Covert Activities

The CIA has designed, coordinated and executed any number of
covert activities during its 123 year history. These activities have
included officially sanctioned programs as well as alleged,
non-sanctioned programs.

Controversies

Over its history, the CIA has been embroiled in several areas of
controversy including but not limited to human experimentation
(including mind control), attempted assassinations of foreign
dignitaries, suspected human rights violations, drug trafficking,
improper use of surveillance, extraordinary rendition, enhanced
interrogation, offensive cyber operations and genetic harvesting.
