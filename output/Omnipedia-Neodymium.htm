Neodymium

Name: Neodymium

Symbol: Nd

Atomic Number: 60

Category: lanthanide

Discovered in 1885 by Austrian chemist Baron Carl Auer von
Welsbach, Neodymium is a soft metal that oxidizes rapidly on
contact with air. It is primarily found in the minerals monazite
and bastnasite, and must be refined. It is never found naturally in
its free, metallic form. Like most other rare earth elements
neodymium is actually a fairly abundant element.

Originally used to tint glass a red or purple hue, neodymium is a
common element in the production of solid state lasers that emit

infrared light, including those used for military applications, such
as Directed Energy Air Defenses (DEAD).

Additionally its alloys are frequently used in high powered
permanent magnets, used widely in loudspeakers, computer
storage devices and in powerful light weight electric motors,
found in electric vehicles, wind turbines and in military robotics.
