TECHNICAL / ARMORED FIGHTING VEHICLE

Technical / AFV : Technical is the name given to a light-armored vehicle that
started out as a civilian vehicle that has been modified to military use with
the addition of armor and frequently an LMG or launcher system.

An Armored Fighting Vehicle is the designation given to similar form factor
vehicles that have been manufactured specifically for combat and
battlefield use.
