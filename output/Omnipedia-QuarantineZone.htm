Quarantine Zone

Following the Coalescence Disaster in 2060, the Singapore
Authorities enacted emergency protocols designed to deal with a
terrorist attack using an unknown virulent pathogen such as
Anthrax or Smallpox, establishing a quarantine zone around the
epicenter of the explosion and subsequent toxic leak.

Initially the quarantine zone was enforced with a series of road
blocks and improvised barricades, with Singapore Defense Forces
personnel policing the crossing points.

As the weeks and months went on, and as efforts to identify the
agents or pathogens that were decimating the population yielded
few results, the perimeter was further re-enforced despite
international protests supporting the plight of those trapped
inside, first with fences and then ultimately with permanent walls
and emplacements.

The quarantine zone encompasses roughly 25 square miles of land
on the east coast of Singapore including former residential areas,
the subterranean 'Science City' tech hub and the Marina Bay
Gardens.

A report published by relief agencies on the one year anniversary
of the disaster noted that death rates amongst the residents of the
quarantine zone were now only 25% higher than those of the
residents of the non-quarantined parts of Singapore, and most of
those due to aggressive tumors and cancers likely caused by
continued exposure to the toxic fallout that blanketed the zone.

Despite these findings, the Singapore Authorities voted
overwhelmingly to maintain the quarantine.

Shortly afterwards reports began to circulate that a well-armed
and well-disciplined criminal combine, the 54 Immortals (54I)
were setting up staging areas inside the quarantine zone, and
sending foraging parties down into the deserted buildings
belonging to the assorted Tech companies that had once operated
there to loot whatever they could find, presumably for sale on the
black-market.

While several skirmishes were reported between the Singapore
Defense Forces and the 54I, they appeared token gestures. It
seems likely that corruption and family ties allowed the 54I easy
access to the quarantine zone.

As supply drops by the international community intended for the
survivors of the disaster began to fall into criminal hands, aid
efforts were suspended.

Citing budgetary problems caused by cleanup operations required
to clean up the damage caused annually by the Super-Storms that
had begun to batter the island nation in 2058, the Singapore
Authorities issued orders to their defense forces not to engage, and
by the second anniversary of the disaster
it was apparent that the 54I had moved their entire criminal
operation into the area, effectively establishing a fiefdom of their
own in the walled enclave.

At the time of the disaster, it was estimated that around 400,000
people were trapped in the newly established quarantine zone.
Two years later there were fewer than 100,000 survivors
remaining.
