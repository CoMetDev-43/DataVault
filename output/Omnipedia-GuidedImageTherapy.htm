Guided Image Therapy

Guided Image Therapy is a guided visualization technique often
used as part of a regimen of Psychotherapy.

During a Guided Image Therapy session, after leading their
patient or patients through a series of focusing breathing and
relaxation exercises, the therapist will begin to describe a place or
a situation that they urge their patients to imagine themselves
within.

The described situation is often tailored to the patient's needs, so
for example a description of a beach and the sound of an ocean
may be effective at reducing stress and anxiety in nervous or
violent patients, whereas patients seeking to overcome a phobia
might be encouraged to think of themselves in a number of
scenarios in which they are facing the object of their fears, while
remaining within a safe environment that they have control over.

Guided Image Therapy has been shown to be effective at reducing
stress, as well as dealing with anxiety, psychological problems and
addictions, as well as in the management of chronic pain.
