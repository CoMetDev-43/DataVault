HOOKAH

This is a water pipe, a device intended for the shared consumption
of flavored tobaccos, which cools the smoke down by bubbling it
through a central chamber containing water before it can be
inhaled.

Enjoyed throughout many Middle Eastern cultures, the Hookah is
thought to have originated in Iran around 1600.

In more peaceful times, it would have been a very common sight
to see people chatting animatedly while sharing a smoke and
enjoying the shade.
