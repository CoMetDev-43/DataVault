WARLORD HELMET

A sophisticated armored helmet complete with control electronics
and enhanced optics, that once was attached to a powered exoskeleton
worn by one of the 54 Immortal's elite 'Warlords'; soldiers with
enhanced specialist training in exo-powered combat.

These exoskeletons are often richly adorned with 54I symbols and
slogans, and given well deserved respect by the rank and file.

As fearsome as these opponents are, they're no match for a
cybernetically enhanced member of the Winslow Accord Special
Forces.

Analysis: Kane, R

If we ever needed direct evidence that these guys are supported by
the CDP, this would be it. That's state-of-the-art CDP exoskeleton
technology. I didn't know they were selling these, I thought they
were only equipping their own shock-troops.
