URN WITH INCENSE STICKS

A small, open topped ceramic urn filled with sand, in which
incense sticks are planted and burnt.

Commonly found in temples, and in home shrines venerating the
families dearly departed.

The Quarantine Zone's de-facto rulers, the 54 Immortals believe
that they are being inducted into a singular family when they
swear their oaths of allegiance, and they venerate the clans fallen,
especially their past leaders, as they would their own ancestors.
