"TIDAL VORTEX" TOPS, "CENTAUR COURT" FLOPS

Ridley Ransom's " Tidal Vortex " remained top of the box office for the second
consecutive week posting a $103M three day total, a 50.3% decline from its
initial $206M opening weekend and increasing its overall haul to a
staggering $309M ($471M worldwide).

Audiences and critics often decry franchises like the Don Summit series, but
with another solid haul for " Tidal Vortex " Sasaki International Pictures
executives have to be giddy about the financial windfall this third entry into
the action adventure series is generating.

The young adult book adaptation "Chrysalis Grove" scored second place
with $53.5M. Apparently the supernatural romance thriller was the perfect
counter-programming to the action heavy Vortex. Those numbers based on
a production budget of only $13.5M.

Although knocked into third place, " Black Lotus Syndicate, " starring K-Pop
sensation Dagger Kang-Hwan, once again surprised in its third week taking
in a healthy $47.5M.

The SFX heavy, creature rom-com, " Centaur Court " about a basketball
playing centaur, flopped domestically, generating only $10.6M on 4,781
screens ($2-217 avg) but fared well overseas with over $81M. With a
production budget of $156M, not to mention tens of millions spent to
promote the creature com, an opening of only $10.6 million, " Centaur Court "
will likely be one of the year's biggest flops.
