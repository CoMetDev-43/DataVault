COALESCENCE CORPORATION CIVILIAN PRODUCTS PROMO
POSTER

These are brightly colored marketing materials for the Coalescence
Corporation 's civilian line of products, found in their corporate
HQ in Zurich.

Despite the colors and the presentation it's becoming increasingly
difficult to tell if their civilian line of robots are descended from
platforms designed for military use, or if the military designs are
built on top of civilian platforms.

The company's materials seem to promise eternal youth and
infinite leisure time to those who can afford it, while
simultaneously challenging the notion that you, the reader,
mightn't be able to.
