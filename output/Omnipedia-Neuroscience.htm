Neuroscience

Neuroscience is an interdisciplinary field that studies the science
of the nervous system.

Encompassing a large number of sub-fields, it studies the anatomy
of the nervous system, from the micro-scale of individual neurons
and their function, up to the macro-scale, looking at the functional
units of the brain and their interconnections.

It interests itself in the biological function of individual neurons,
and how they are developmentally affected by their genetics, as
well as in the electrical function of those neurons, through the
study of electrophysiology.

Neuroscientists and psychologists work together to discover the
underlying biological or structural causes of a wide range of
psychiatric conditions such as schizophrenia, in order to discover
novel treatments for them.
