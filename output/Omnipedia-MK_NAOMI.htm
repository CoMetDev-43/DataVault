MKNAOMI

Functional from the 1950's through 1969, MKNAOMI was a
classified program run by the United States Department of
Defense and the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA). The program
was reported to have specifically explored strengthening U.S.
biological weapon capabilities as well as developing
countermeasures to those weapons.

The full scope of MKNAOMI is not completely known, as
declassified information has been scarce. Investigators have
reported that the program was the successor to the MKDELTA
program, which involved mind control and interrogation.

In 1967, during a U.S. Senate committee investigation, documents
were discovered detailing various covert operations related to

MKNAOMI, including the development of a weapon that fired
special darts coated with biological or chemical agents, covert
techniques on poisoning crops and research into biological agents
that could be used against animals.

In 1969, a U.S. Presidential order abolished the use of biological
weapons and MKNAOMI was dissolved.
