CDP SELECTS NEW ASSAULT RIFLES

The Common Defense Pact (CDP) has selected an assault rifle as the new
standard issue firearm as part of its soldier modernization program.

"We have already have selected our assault rifles. It was chosen due to its
effectiveness and its low cost. We find the quality is also acceptable." CDP
commander Yuriy Anenih stated to the press on 21 October.

Yuriy stated that two assault rifles were selected by the CDP: the KN-44,
chambered in 5.45x39 mm, and the 7.62x39 mm.

"We will now increase the number of ordered systems from 75,000 to
100,000", Yuriy added.

The KN-44 design takes cues from the open source KN Weapons Platform,
that has been printed and constantly updated for the last twenty years.

According to Yuriy, the KN-44 maintains compatibility with many of the
attachments available for earlier KN weapons, and they will slot into the
KN-44 without modification.

This was crucial as many of the CDP soldiers have been using locally
sourced KN-42A3 and KN-43A3 rifles for many years.

The KN-44 is a seventh generation KN pattern rifle, featuring many
improvements, a redesigned bolt catch, customizable accessory rails, and
an optional telescopic butt stock.

The KN-44 also has a lower recoil impulse than the previous version.

Work on the design of the KN-44 began in July 2062 with the first prototype
released on 24 September 2062. The rifle is fed from standard 30 round KN
box magazines.

The rifle features advanced biometric security that prevents un-authorized
use, greatly enhancing the safety features of this platform.

In December, the CDP announced that all the rifles had passed the initial
trial program, and will be fielding the weapon by the end of the year.
