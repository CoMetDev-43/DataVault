Taos Hum

The Taos Hum or simply "the hum" is a series of phenomena that
involve a persistent low-frequency humming that is not audible to
all people.

The name originates from the town of Taos, New Mexico where
the hum was first reported in the 1990's. According to a report out
of the University of New Mexico, the hum can only be heard by 2%
of the general population.

Although highly publicized, the hum has been heard in many
places outside of Taos, New Mexico. Mystery sounds reportedly
similar to the hum have been reported from Borneo (2012) to
Coventry, England (2014).

Possible Explanations

Explanations for the Taos Hum range from sound emanating
from heavy industrial mechanical devices to a range of potentially
auditory or nervous system disorders that may actually cause a
person to hear faint buzzing or ringing sounds.

There is still speculation from the scientific community on
whether the phenomenon even exists.

In Popular Culture

Since there hasn't been a single identifiable source associated with
the Taos Hum, there have been a variety of theories offered up as
an explanation. These theories range from the ordinary to
extraordinary, from the psychological to the paranormal; secret
underground UFO bases run by the illuminati to government
mind control programs run by the CIA and everything in between
have been blamed for the hum.
