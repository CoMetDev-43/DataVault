Abasi Mineral Inc.

Abasi Mineral Inc. or AMI is a Kenyan mining corporation based
out of Malindi, Kenya. Owned and Operated by Abasi Hakim and
his two brothers Adhama & Hamisi.

Created in 2027, AMI served as one of the largest local deep
mining units specializing in rare mineral deposits (see also rare
earth elements ). Current estimates have AMI accounting for 18%
of the world’s annual supply of niobium and 23% of the world's
supply of neodymium.

AMI’s total net revenue is currently unknown but is estimated to
be into the 600M range by some industry analysts.
