Noise Ordinance

Rabiah and Nanu Nahas from the 27th floor have proposed a new noise
ordinance that Akil and I very much support.

The last few nights have seen quite a few Army soldiers from various
outposts spending late nights in the 2nd floor commissary.

While we proudly support our local armed forces, some of them have gotten
extremely rowdy near the front entrance and smoking areas.

Please join us in signing the noise ordinance and petition.
