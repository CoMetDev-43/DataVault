KwK 43

The KwK-43 (Kampfwagenkanone-43) was an 88 mm tank gun
designed by Krupp to serve as the main gun of the Tiger II heavy
tank, in service with the German army during the Second World
War.

At 6.2 meters in length, the KwK-43 was a full 1.3 meters longer
than that used in the earlier Tiger I tank. This allowed for the
usage of heavier propellant charges giving the Tiger II a
substantial range advantage, as well as granting it substantially
higher rates of armor penetration.

It was capable of firing a 10.4kg shell at a muzzle velocity of 1000
m/s to a range of 4000 meters.
