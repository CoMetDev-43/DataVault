Shadow Government (Conspiracy)

The term Shadow Government is often associated with the secret
or invisible government that populates conspiracy oriented
literature, television shows and motion pictures.

The term originates from the idea that actual political power does
not reside with the publicly elected representatives but rather
with private individuals who exercise power behind the scenes
without the scrutiny of a democratically elected institution. The
shadow government in actuality is the true executive power while
the officially elected government is essentially a subservient
facade.

In Popular Culture

The term Shadow Government has been popularized by various
television shows, movies and literature. Often, the portrayal is rife
with various conspiracy theories that involve known or unknown
government organizations, financial institutions or secret
societies engaging in covert activities.

These conspiracy theories range broadly from extraterrestrial
aliens who control the human race via a secret inner circle of
illuminati to other organizations like the CIA who want to
establish a new world order.
