RUSSIAN FIELD COMPASS

A printed reproduction of a Russian wrist-worn field compass,
found in a depiction of a Second World War battlefield in an

Immersion experienced with Sarah Hall.

Known as an Adrianov compass, it was first developed in 1907 by
Russian cartographer Vladimir Nikolaevich Adrianov (b. 1875, d.
1938) for military service, and saw widespread deployment with
the Red Army and later Soviet troops.

Despite being wrist worn, it was reportedly quite awkward to use.

The markings on this one suggest that it was made in Russia in
1940.
