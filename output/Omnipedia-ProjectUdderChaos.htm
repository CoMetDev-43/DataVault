Project Udder Chaos

Project Udder Chaos, also known by the media as Project
Moo-nitions, was a clandestine CIA program operating near the
Bourgogne region of France that took place around 2018.

The program was an attempt to train and weaponize Charolais
cattle and Markhor goats by surgically implanting various pieces
of explosive material into their bodies.

The goal of the program was to create an animal based bomb
delivery platform capable of reaching insurgent populations in
what the CIA deemed "high density terrain."

Discovery

On May 23, 2027, Henri Favager discovered classified
documentation on the perimeter of a farm near the city of
Saint-Fargeau. The documentation recovered on the farm alluded
to CIA and French government participation in a clandestine
program involving surgical procedures to create an animal based
bomb delivery platform.

Much of the information reported has been disputed as the
documentation recovered was either transferred during the initial
investigation to the United States or reported lost by French
Police. Documents still in existence were sealed during a
subsequent U.S. congressional inquiry.

According to a report by the French magazine, Oiseau de Paradis,
an anonymous source inside the French National Assembly stated
that the project was allegedly a cousin of the CIA's Acoustic Kitty

project.

Congressional Inquiry and Leaks

On June 12, 2028, a congressional inquiry was launched and the
CIA came under increased scrutiny about the validity of the
project with multiple allegations of animal cruelty as well as
misappropriation of agency funds.

Leaked congressional reports detailing the exploits of the program
were released in 2033 by a Belgian hacker group known as Druon.
According to the reports, the cattle used in the program were
trained extensively, implanted with tracking and remote stimulus
devices monitored by the CIA. The cattle also underwent
extensive surgery to replace portions of their ocular cavity with
high definition retinal cameras equipped with recording and
broadcast capabilities. Additionally, the cattle were also implanted
with 2 to 3lbs of general-purpose explosive material. These
explosives were sewn into areas just above the animal's udder.

The leaked reports stated that after rigorous training the cattle
would then be shipped or parachute dropped into regions
inaccessible to ground troops. The animals would then be directed
by remote stimulus to infiltrate opposition strongholds. The CIA
or military personnel monitoring these cattle would then
determine if targets in the perimeter were of value and detonate
the explosives sewn inside the animals. These explosives were
rumored to be powerful enough to demolish a two-story building.

After a number of failed test exercises, the program was retooled,
and the Charolais cattle were replaced by Markhor goats.
Documentation contained in the Druon leak suggests that this was
due to the goat's superior ability to climb to higher altitudes,
ability to scale or burrow under fences and their rapid response
time to remote brain stimulus.

In 2021, the program was cancelled due to lack of funding.
