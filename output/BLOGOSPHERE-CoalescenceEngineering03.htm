Level I - Applied Nanoengineering

Month 1

Met some interesting folks in the commissary today.

Bill an older gentleman from mechatronics, I think he's from the mid-west
because he's always wearing a plaid shirt under his lab coat. Not sure how
long he's been here, but he seems really experienced and has that grandpa
like air about him which makes everyone feel instantly comfortable when
he's around.

[PERSONNEL FILE - Bill H. Dawtry]

Next to him was a wiry guy with glasses named Fredrik. Freddy, as he likes to
be called, is Swiss acoustical engineer who's into dressage. Yeah, I don't
understand it either but apparently his family has been a member of the
confederation of international equine dressage since 1923. He and Bill are
connected at the hip, I guess it's sort of funny that even though these guys
grew up worlds apart and have a twenty year age difference, they connect
about their love of equestrian sport.

Freddy mentioned that he was just on loan from our sister facility in Zurich
for the next six months.

[PERSONNEL FILE - Fredrik S. Krieger]

Met a really nice girl named Jayme from the biochemical systems
department. She's from some small town outside of Sydney. One stop light
type of place. She has the coolest accent. I've asked her to repeat so many
words, just to hear it... over and over again. I guess that's why I like her so
much.

[PERSONNEL FILE - Jayme E. Pryer]

I also met the tallest Latvian in existence, Juris from optical engineering.
Okay, okay, he's the first Latvian I've ever met but he's also like 6ft7 so you
can't blame me for making a joke about it. Apparently his brother was on
the Latvian Olympic team.

[PERSONNEL FILE - Juris K. Strau]

Nancy from the inorganic chemistry lab is probably the most analytical of
the group. She's really quiet and is allergic to nearly everything. She's the
only person I've ever heard of having aquagenic urticarial - yep- she's
allergic to water. I guess that's why she spent most of her youth indoors and
studying. She's brilliant but definitely odd.

[PERSONNEL FILE - Nancy L. Chiu]

We've all become fast friends this week. Jayme jokingly referred to us as the
"lunch bunch" because we've been sitting with one another for a good
portion of the week. I guess we're creating our own little clique.

So yeah, we're quite the menagerie of oddities, but then that's what you get
when you recruit the best and brightest from around the world.
