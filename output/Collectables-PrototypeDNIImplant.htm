PROTOTYPE DNI IMPLANT

An early version Direct Neural Interface (DNI) implant found in
the skeleton of a test subject, deep in the hidden labs underneath
the Coalescence Corporation facility in Singapore.

The unit is bulky compared to the ones used in the Winslow Accord
Cyber-Soldier program. It is marked with a manufacture date of
2058.

Analysis: Kane, R

That's one ugly augmentation. Looks like it was externally
powered, while the wiring we saw in the lab would suggest that it
lacked any of the high-bandwidth near-field communications
systems that you guys use to interface with external systems.

The surgery to install it must have been pretty invasive; the
recovery time would have been significant, and it wouldn't
function outside of the laboratory.

It also doesn't have any of the biocompatibility coatings that yours
do. They must have either been suppressing the test-subjects'
immune systems, or just not expecting them to survive long
enough for rejection to be an issue.

Who would have consented to test one of these?
