The Locus "Version 09" (v.09) is one of the many production
variations in a series of highly customizable rifles which combine
the latest in materials engineering with a time-proven, reliable
bolt-action design.

The Locus series has been engineered to minimize environmental
variables such as temperature and humidity to guarantee accuracy
in the most extreme operating environments. While there are
many versions of the Locus rifle, any of them can be quickly
identified by their unique twisted barrel design.

The Locus v.09 is built for portability and mobility, making it an
excellent choice for operating on the move over rough terrain.
Although reliant on a bolt-action operation, ergonomic and
mechanical optimizations allow for quick, smooth cycling with
minimal movement of the rifle itself.

The Locus v.09 is chambered for a high velocity round to reduce
the time from trigger pull to round impact over long distance.
Impact is typically lethal so long as it collides with bone to deliver
maximum energy near a target's vitals, which in most cases
translates to "hit anything in the ribs and above," as most shooters
are taught when learning the weapons platform.

Coupled with a ballistics computer and good optics, the Locus is
capable of unwavering accuracy and reliable performance under
extreme conditions.
