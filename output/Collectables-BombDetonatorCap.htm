BOMB DETONATOR CAP

This is a 3D printed replica of an electrically initiated blasting cap,
found while immersed in the Cyber-soldier training simulation.

The scenario was based on a historical incident in which terrorists
were intent on attacking the interests of the Coalescence
Corporation, the company responsible for the supply of much of
our battlefield robotics, as well as the technology behind the bulk
of our cybernetic implants and our DNI systems.

Comprised of an electronically triggered ignition charge and a
small quantity of high explosive, encased in an aluminum tube,
they are used to initiate explosions in larger quantities of stable,
military grade high explosives.
