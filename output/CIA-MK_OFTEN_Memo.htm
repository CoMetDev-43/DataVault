DATE: 20 July 1962

MEMORANDUM FOR: Technical Service Branch Chiefs

SUBJECT: Use of Agency Recruitment funds

Gentlemen,

It has been brought to my attention that we have spent over $67,534 on
"recruiting events" across the country this year.

While I understand the need for a substantial "cover story" for this particular
operation, I would recommend curtailing expenditures for the remainder of
the year.

If the chief finds out how much funding is being diverted to recruiting events
involving "astrologists, fortune tellers and clairvoyants" we'll be the folks
testing the next batch of EA 3167.
