Masud Assaf

Success 21.04.64

I'm sorry it's been so long since I have written.

I am currently resting in a medical unit situated within a converted railway
station.

Mother please do not worry, but I was wounded recently. I was lucky and
received only some minor burns and scrapes from an I.E.D that was planted
outside of a small house my unit was walking past. I was very lucky to have
my friend Ottah by my side, thanks to his quick thinking and a hard push to
the left I am still alive.

The burns cover half of my chest and right arm, but they were very much
worth it as we came back with the body of the enemy sniper who took Yazid.

I will write more in a few days, the medicine they have given me makes me
sleep and the nurses keep telling me that I cannot stay at the computer
terminal all day.

I am going to rest.
