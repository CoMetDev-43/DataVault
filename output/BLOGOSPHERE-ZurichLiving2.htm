My 16th Birthday

So today was like the best day EVER!

475 of my closest friends were at our villa today to surprise me for my sweet 16! They
all had on the cutest costumes too! Daddy knows how much I love animals, so
everyone had on adorable animal hats and body suits. Tigers, bunnies, cheetahs! So
stinkin' cute!

The food was amazing too. Daddy had the chefs make 25 different hors d'oeuvres.

Crips parmesan baskets filled with goat cheese, clams with lemon cream and caviar,
phyllo bites with asparagus and prosciutto, sea scallops wrapped in pancetta, crab
cake lollipops, seared peppercorn chicken satay, baby lamb chops... those were just
a few of my favorites! YUM! #FoodieFatty.

The dessert tables were even better. Seven tables of pure sugar!!!

And the food wasn't even the best part!

Daddy got me a CAR! It's a hot pink, electric L730! 730 horsepower and 509lb-ft of
torque with a dual clutch seven speed automated manual transmission. It can go
60mph from a standing start in just over three seconds and can reach up to 211
miles per hour!!!! {Bold is daddy details, I don't know much about cars. Thanks for the
info daddy!}

Daddy says it's only one of sixteen in existence and that he special ordered it four
years ago from the dealer in downtown Zurich. Now, I just need to learn how to
drive!! :-)

See you on the roads bitches! #ZurichElitePrepClassof70
