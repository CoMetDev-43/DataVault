CIA Cryptonyms

Digraphs and Cryptonyms

Cryptonyms, or code-words are assigned to Top Secret, or Above
Top Secret CIA projects, or to certain high-value individuals to
obfuscate their identities.

A CIA cryptonym is composed of two parts, a prefix of 2 characters
known as a digraph, and a following dictionary word.

Sometimes the digraph and word form a word of their own, i.e.
AEROPLANE or DIURNAL.

Names are usually chosen from a 'sterile list', so that the name
chosen doesn't give away anything about the project that it covers.

The digraph generally specifies the geographical area, or
organization affected by the project.

Cryptonyms are sometimes written with a / separating the digraph
from the rest of the code-word, e.g. MK/ULTRA vs MKULTRA.

Not all projects will receive a true cryptonym.
