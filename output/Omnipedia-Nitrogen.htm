Nitrogen

Name: Nitrogen

Symbol: N

Atomic Number: 7

Category: diatomic nonmetal

Nitrogen is a colorless and odorless diatomic gas; it is the seventh
most abundant element in our galaxy. The element forms 78% of
earth's atmosphere and is the most abundant pure element. Nitrogen
was discovered as a component of air, by Scottish physician
Daniel Rutherford, in 1772.

Nitrogen occurs in all known organisms, it is found in amino
acids, nucleic acids (DNA and RNA), and proteins; 3% of the mass of
a human body is Nitrogen, it being the fourth most abundant
element in the body.
