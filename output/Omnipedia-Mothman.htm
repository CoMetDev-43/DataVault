Mothman

The Mothman is a creature that was encountered in the Point
Pleasant area of West Virginia from November 1966 to December
1967.

The first recoded sighting occurred near Clendenin, West Virginia.
Five men at the Clendenin Cemetery reported seeing a man-like
figure fly between the trees over their head while digging a grave
after dusk. They did not report this sighting until after the
newspapers detailed the account of two young couple's interaction
with an owl-like man with red glowing eyes on the road near what
was called "the TNT area", the site of a former World War II
munitions plant.

Over the following weeks multiple sightings of what the
newspapers dubbed the Mothman were reported to the Point
Pleasant Police department. The sightings ended after the collapse
of the Silver Bridge on December 15, 1967, giving rise to legends
that the Mothman and the bridge collapse were connected.
