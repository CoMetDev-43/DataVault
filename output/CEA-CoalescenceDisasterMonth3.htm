COALESCENCE DISASTER - THREE MONTHS LATER

09/02/2060 - SINGAPORE

3 months have passed since tragedy struck at the site of the former

Coalescence Corporation Research and Development facility in Singapore,
their once proud building now a ruined monument to the memory of the
250,000 confirmed to have died at this time.

Clean up work, care for the survivors now trapped within the Singapore

Quarantine Zone and the search for the toxin, agent or pathogen that
caused the greatest loss of civilian life in recorded history is still under way.

Reports from the scientists within the Quarantine Zone show that the fatality
rate has dropped dramatically, although alarmingly they are starting to see
deaths from aggressive cancers as well as from conventional, easily treated
illnesses that are overwhelming the survivors ravaged immune systems.

Members of the Coalescence Corporations ' board of directors began giving
testimony at closed hearings today, whose goal is to uncover cause and
culpability for the disaster. The hearings are expected to last at least six
months, although many pundits predict they could drag on for years.

Singapore Authorities have begun a large scale building project intended to
replace the temporary structures currently encircling the Quarantine Zone.
An encircling wall and a series of guard towers are planned.

Opponents of the plan say that the authorities are effectively creating a
vast, open-air prison; shutting the survivors away out of sight and out of
mind. The authorities assert that despite the cost of the construction, the
new Quarantine Zone walls will be substantially cheaper to man than the
porous system of roadblocks that exist today.
