Druon

Druon or Druon Antigoon (xx_DA_xx) is an underground Belgium
based hacker collective that was formed around 2030.

Druon's total membership is unknown, but speculation from
various intelligence services estimates it to be anywhere between
50 and 100 members with a mix of grey hat and black hat hackers.
All of its members are presumably based in or around Western
Europe, with its key leadership likely based in the city of Antwerp,
Belgium.

They sprang to notoriety in 2037 when they leaked information
stolen from the CDP s' central databases showing that they were
dealing with the NRC several years before the NRC became
signatories of that Mutual Defense Pact.

Little is known about Druon's other activities apart from their
participation in the release of over 60,000 pages of classified
documentation between 2030 and 2035, the most notable of which
being classified briefs from the joint CIA and French operation
known as Project Udder Chaos.

Origin of the name

The group's name, Druon Antigoon, was taken from a mythical
giant that inhabited ancient Antwerp. According to local legend,
the giant charged a hefty toll to cross the river Schedlt. If the toll
was not paid the giant severed the hands of those who refused and
threw them into the river below.
