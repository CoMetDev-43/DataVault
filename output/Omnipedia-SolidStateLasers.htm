Solid State Lasers

Solid State Lasers are lasers that use a solid active laser medium -
the part where light is amplified by 'pumping' energy from an
external source, such as laser diodes, rather than a liquid or a gas
based medium.

The solid medium used is frequently a glass or crystalline
substrate that has been 'doped' with a rare-earth element, such as

neodymium.

Because Solid State Lasers are capable of firing either very rapid
pulses, or sustained beams they are the laser-of-choice for many
industrial applications, such as drilling, welding and cutting.

The doped glasses or ceramics at the heart of Solid State Lasers can
be used at very high energy levels, which make them ideal
candidates for pumping fusion reactions or for use in Directed
Energy Air Defense systems.
