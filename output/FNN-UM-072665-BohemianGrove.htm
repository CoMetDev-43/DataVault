SHADOW GOVERNMENT - BOHEMIAN GROVE

"Anybody can be President of the United States, but very few can ever have
any hope of becoming President of the Bohemian Club."

- President Richard Nixon, 1972.

The Bohemian Grove is a 2,700-acre redwood grove in Northern
California, a few hours north of San Francisco, where the rich and
powerful visit with each other during the last two weeks of July. For
almost two hundred years the Bohemian Club has been the secret seat
of power in the United States.

Closed to the public, sealed off by a private security force and armed
guards, the power elite engage in activities that change the course of
world politics at the Grove. Whether it's crafting policy matters in
secret, dancing around in forest, finalizing deals with Reptoids,
practicing black magic or even committing murder, the pristine
isolation of the Bohemian Grove provides all the fitting concealment
for any immoral, or illegal behavior that one needs.
