HALL, SARAH
DOB: 06/06/2033
POB: Los Angeles, CA, USA.

Orphaned age 7. Ward of State, spends 5 years transferring from
foster home to foster home before being adopted.

Graduates high-school 2051, joins military aged 18 - answers
recruiter question "Why are you joining the service", with "To be
part of something larger. To find a family."

Armed Services Vocational Aptitude Battery scores suggest Special
Forces Training. Q-Course completion in 85 weeks, with specialty
training in Communications Systems. Grades in courses
concerned with historical engagements and tactics exceptional.

2052, joins US Army 1st Special Forces Group (SFG),
headquartered out of the Joint Base Lewis-McChord, Washington.

Assigned to the Combined Joint Special Operations Task Force -
Arabian Peninsula.

2058 seconded to Winslow Accord Special Forces - Serves as liaison
and training officer to Egyptian Special Forces in Cairo.

2060 returns to USA for a memorial service for her adoptive
parents, who were caught up in the Coalescence Disaster in
Singapore.

2063 critically injured in NRC attack on Cairo.

Accepted Project Prometheus, 2064.
