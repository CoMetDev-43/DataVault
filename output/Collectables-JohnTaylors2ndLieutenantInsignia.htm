JOHN TAYLOR'S 2ND LIEUTENTANT INSIGNIA

This small gold bar lapel pin is the 2nd Lieutenant Insignia
awarded to John Taylor when he graduated West Point Military
Academy in 2041.

He was an exemplary cadet and an outstanding soldier who
moved quickly through the ranks and entered Special Forces
training.

This insignia must have meant something special to him to have
kept it with him like this. It's hard to imagine what kind of duress
Taylor must have been under in order to betray his own so
completely.
