DECORATIVE HANGING LATERN

This 'fanous', or decorative lamp was found hanging in what had
once been one of the nicer apartments in Lotus Towers.

It's a traditional lamp, whose origin dates back to around 910AD
when the people of Cairo are said to have lined the streets carrying
them, to welcome the arrival of the Caliph.

Analysis: Kane, R

Clearly mass-produced and sold in bulk for decorating large
apartment blocks. I did a little digging on the apartment that you
found it in. It turns out that the original buyers of those
apartments had an online community, before the mega-structures
were turned into refugee camps.
