Illuminati

Illuminati (latin illuminatus "enlightened") is a term most

commonly associated with an organization purported to have

links to various secret societies who conspire to control the world.

They are generally depicted as a shadowy group pulling the levers

of power and are often cited as a critical piece of some of the most

elaborate and pervasive conspiracy theories in history.

Although the name illuminati has often been attributed to various

groups throughout history, the modern day association is usually

with that of an organization involved in conspiring to control

world affairs by seeding agents into various governments or

corporations in order to exert power and influence in an effort to

establish a new world order.

In Popular Culture

Modern day conspiracy theorists often attribute to the illuminati

the organization of significant world events that have had major

political or social ramifications. This list includes everything from

the Battle of Waterloo to the alleged CIA backed assassination of

US President John F. Kennedy.

Some theorists claim that the illuminati are involved in

everything from occult rituals to manipulation of the stock market

through the banking industry.

One popular conspiracy theory mentions that the illuminati
operate in the Mt. Weather facility in the United States and are
one half of the world's secret Shadow Government.
