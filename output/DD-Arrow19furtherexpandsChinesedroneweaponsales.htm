ARROW 19 FURTHER EXPANDS CHINESE DRONE WEAPON SALES

The two Chinese companies leading this competition are Nayetti and the
China Aerospace Technology Corporation (CAETC), which have unveiled
several new missiles for drones in recent years.

Nayetti's Arrow 16 lightweight, air-launched, ground-attack missile was
revealed in 2052. Later that year, the Blaze Arrow 16, which weighs 37kg
and uses semi-active laser guidance, was seen integrated on the attack
VTOLs and has subsequently been seen in use during Army exercises.

Nayetti revealed its Blaze Arrow 19 at Shongshan 2058 airshow. At 22.5 kg,
the much-lighter missile is the company's attempt to address the drone
weapons market. It uses a semi-active laser guidance system and has a
range of 12 kilometres.

While it is not yet confirmed, the Blaze Arrow 16/19 likely arms the Coalescence
Corporation Wraith drones that have reportedly been sold to Winslow Accord
signatories.
