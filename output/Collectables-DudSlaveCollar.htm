DUD SLAVE COLLAR

This unpleasant device is a common place 'tool' used in the 54
Immortals human-trafficking operations. The titanium collar
contains a small quantity of military grade explosives. Once
fastened around a person's neck, those explosives are armed.

The collar can be detonated remotely, or can be geo-fenced such
that if it strays outside of a given area, after a series of warning
beeps it takes the head of the victim clean off.

They also feature a failsafe, detonating in the event that anyone
tries to open the collar without the correct tools.
