National Clandestine Service

The National Clandestine Service (NCS) is one of four major
organizational components of the Central Intelligence Agency. Its
primary purpose is to coordinate, collect and organize foreign
intelligence data gathered from human intelligence activities. This
includes data gathering from the wider U.S. intelligence
organization.

The NCS engages in various covert operations including but not
limited to offensive counterespionage, propaganda, support of
foreign paramilitary units and psychological operations. These
covert operations are often intended to propel the United States
government's political, economic or military agendas across the
globe.
