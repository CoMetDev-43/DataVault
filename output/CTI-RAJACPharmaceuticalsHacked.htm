RAJAC PHARMACEUTICALS HACKED

Author: Matthew Burns

Date: Aug 2058

Hot on the heels of the Updog Savings and Loan hack, another corporation's internal
network has been compromised. This time the RAJAC (Reynolds Anderson Joint American
Corporation) Pharmaceuticals Division was the target. The goals and extent of the
security breach is not yet known. RAJAC has access to the national health care database,
which would include the personal information of every tax payer. We have contacted
RAJAC for more information about the incident and will update as news breaks.

<update>

Members of the hacker group Druon have claimed responsibility. The Belgium based
hacker group sent an update @druonAntigoon "RAJAC's hand was tossed into the river".
