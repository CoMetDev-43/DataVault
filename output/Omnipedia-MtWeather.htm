Mt. Weather

The Mount Weather Emergency Operations Center is a facility
located in the state of Virginia within the United States of America.

In use since 1959, the facility currently acts as one of the major
continuity of government operating sites that high ranking
civilian and military officials may use in the case of a national
emergency.

Located in the Blue Ridge Mountains, the facility is only 48 miles
from Washington, D.C. and contains facilities above and below
ground.

Reports indicate that those facilities may contain training areas,
barracks and control stations for the federal emergency alert
system.

In Popular Culture

Mt. Weather has appeared in various television shows and
movies. It is often mentioned as part of various conspiracy
theories including but not limited to: a secret CIA training facility,
the seat of the Shadow Government and headquarters of the

illuminati.
