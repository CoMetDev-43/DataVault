DECORATIVE ETCHED GLASS BOTTLE

Small, brightly colored hand-blown glass bottles like these have
been manufactured in Egypt for hundreds of years, using stained
glass coloring techniques pioneered there thousands of years ago.

These bottles are mostly manufactured for the tourist trade
attracted to Egypt's perfume industry, which mostly wholesales
raw perfume oils to Europe's largest perfume houses.

The use of perfume and it's manufacture are recording in glyphs
and friezes found in ancient Egyptian tombs, although until the
Persian scholar Avicenna discovered the process of distilling the
natural oils from flowers in around 1000AD, those perfumes used
to be a suspension of crushed flowers and spices in oil.
