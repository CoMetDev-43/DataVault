NARCIS DRAVINKSI FALL LINE MAKES A SPLASH IN MILAN

By Mossimo Muzzi

Fashion Desk - Europe

Milan, Italy - Updated 8:40pm ET

After curating some of the most spectacular venues in recent memory --
who could forget the glacial crystals of the bamboo forest, the mesmerizing
holographic tomb of Cleopatra and - let us not ignore the decadent whimsy
of the candy infused Roman bath house - Narcis Dravinski, the Croatian
Count of Couture produced a fall palate cleanser.

And amazingly enough, it was the right choice: His new " ELABOR8 MZFIT "
couture line was a superb orgy of craftsmanship, over the top
ornamentation and dastardly decadent detail. The focus this season was
clearly on a stunning, career defining miracle of modernity and not the P.T.
Barnum like showmanship of his previous collections.

A mix of " robotic-chic " and " Antebellum punk," Dravinski's fall line
incorporates shiny wearables along with traditional leathers and flamboyant
decorative chiffon.

" Elabor8 MZFIT is a revelation and blends the unique technical style of
today's street youth with the decadence of the Antebellum South," said
fashion icon Kingsley Mire.

"The future with baroque decadence!" Dravinski yelped as he presented his
17 model runway show on the lip of the Piazza de Baggio. "Elabor8 MZFIT
will revolutionize couture just like " Versailles Reclamation " did with bourgeois
chic!"

Unlike Dravinski's " Reclamation " line which involved Japanese salvage denim,
pleated surcoats and antique tasseled courtesan gauntlets the " MZFIT " line
has aesthetics of architectural purity - stiff metal tech bracelets, clean yet
voluminous A-line dresses and neoprene hoop skirts with elaborate but
tasteful sequin patterns.

The accompanying accessory collection ran the gauntlet of fashion, from
spongy leather satchel bags to rockabilly brooches inlaid with Portuguese
diamonds and the piece de resistance, a dazzling statement choker with
parallel piping in a deep fallow and chalky fuchsia.

In homage perhaps to Apollonian " Kunsttriebe," the show segued from
accessories into a plethora of masculine " Heracles " suits outfitted with
eighteenth century embroidery, flaming Falu red tweed with bristling xanadu
green plastic filament along with hand sewn black rattlesnake stitching set
into richly detailed textile works that resembled the surreal imagery of Dutch
painter Sophia VanderGoth's " Smoldering embers of a lava flow in the Ninth
World."

As the beautiful show came to a finale and the models swirled onstage like a
couture cyclone, Dravinski appeared to rousing applause with his blushing
bride to be Octavia Lamarche, clad in her breathtakingly daring faux
vampire chic, decadent Malachite eye jewelry and hand woven Brazilian
banana leaf parasol dyed in a stunning tint of gothic Arsenic.

Gliding off the runaway, droves of onlookers rushed to congratulate the
auteur on another immaculate collection, Dravinski was as sanguine as ever.
"If I were to disappear tomorrow, Elabor8 MZFIT would be the
representation of my total self. This movement is as true to who Narcis
Dravinski is as an artist as there ever was or ever will be... or at least until
next year's collection," he added with his signature whimsical smirk.
