INTERACTIVE RAIL MAP

This is a 3D printed replica of a rail map found in the Cyber-soldier
training Immersion's representation of the Zurich Mag Lev train
system.

The real thing is a triumph of miniaturization and cost reduction,
acting as both a season pass to the cities advanced Mag Lev
transport system, as well as an interactive map.

The tickets integrated touch-sensitive e-ink displays allowing the
owner to plan journeys as well as track their current journey's
progress and share ETA's with their contacts in the event of a
delay.

Analysis: Kane, R

Only in Zurich. It's not like those things are that expensive to
produce - but the town likes to wear its tech-credentials on its
sleeve.
