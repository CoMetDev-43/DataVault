DEFILED NRC PROPAGANDA POSTER

This NRC produced poster is typical of the propaganda plied by an
occupying army. It and others like it claim that the NRC is here to
protect citizens from poor mistakes made by their own
governments that brought about the current situation.

They urge calm, obedience and the reporting of any 'militant
resistance elements' that friends or neighbors may be harboring.

In return for calm and co-operation, they promise the regular
supply of clean, drinkable water, the very resource that has
brought so much suffering to the region in recent years.

That irony hasn't been lost on the local inhabitants, and the spirit
of rebellion is alive and well. The poster has been defaced with
anti-NRC slogans. Many such acts of civil disobedience have
occurred all over Cairo.
