WW2 US Field Radio

A reproduction of an SCR-694 field radio, widely used by the US
Military during the Second World War.

While listed as portable, the vacuum tube based transmitter
weighed 19.5 pounds, without power source.

The unit was usually powered by a generator, although a hand
cranked portable generator, complete with a fold-out seat was
available.

When we encountered it in the Immersion, it was playing the
following transmission.

Analysis: Kane, R

Crazy that wars were won and lost with these things. It's hardly
portable, and would have only had a range of around 15 miles. The AM
band that it broadcast on would also have been susceptible to
atmospheric conditions, and as far as I can tell - there's no concept
of encryption or secure channels.
