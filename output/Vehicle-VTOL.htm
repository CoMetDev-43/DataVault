VERTICAL TAKE-OFF AND LANDING

VTOLs are the standard workhorse aircraft in modern military usage today.
There are a number of variants deployed by different factions, provided by
different manufacturers.

Different vehicles have different internal layouts; some intended as troop
transports - others as mobile command facilities.

A number of different weapon load outs are available for these vehicles, but
their limited agility favors standoff and over-horizon weapon types.
