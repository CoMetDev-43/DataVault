Tulpa

Tulpa is a Tibetan word for a magical construct created through
spiritual and metal discipline. The word Tulpa can be translated
as "spiritual emanation", "conjured thing" or "phantom". It is
defined as any unreal, illusory or mind-made body In Indian
Buddhist traditions.

On the 21st March 2062 the 15th Dalai Lama at a teaching event in
Brisbane, Australia was asked what would be the spiritual
significance of the first truly sentient Artificial Intelligence. His
reply stated that that a true A.I. would be another form of Tulpa,
and that the teachings associated with Tulpas would apply to this
new thought form, once free of its makers control.
