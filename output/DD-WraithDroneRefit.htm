WRAITH DRONE REFIT

The Zurich defense force expanded their order on retrofitted Wraith Drones
produced by the Coalescence Corporation this week. "With the rapid
technological advances needed on the battlefield we find purchasing
retrofitted drones suits our needs, as well as the needs of the people we
serve." said Lt. Schafer the Defense Force commander.

The ZDF will be receiving an additional 65 units by the end of the year.

Zurich is one of a handful of countries that use such hardware for domestic
law enforcement purposes. When they receive the latest shipment along with
their new security robots, they will have the highest number of drones in
service per capita in the world.

The new drones will fulfil roles in search and rescue as well as law
enforcement patrol and civilian monitoring.
