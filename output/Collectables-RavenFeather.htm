Raven Feather

This is a 3D printed representation of a flight feather from a Common
Raven ( Corvus corax ) found in an Immersion in Zurich. The
vanes, barbs and barbules are perfectly detailed; you can separate
and smooth them with your fingers, as you would a real feather.
Functionally, there's no reason why a feather in a simulation
would be modelled to such accuracy.

The Common Raven is the most widely distributed member of the
Raven family, found across the entire Northern Hemisphere.

Ravens have been found to be highly intelligent birds, with a
penchant for problem solving and are rapid learners. They have
been observed fashioning tools from leaves and twigs, which they
use in food gathering activities, such as using a rudimentary hook
made from the spars of a leaf to extract grubs from rotten logs.
