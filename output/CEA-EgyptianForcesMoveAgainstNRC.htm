BREAKING NEWS: EGYPTIAN FORCES MOVE AGAINST NRC

CAIRO

In a daring, perhaps last-ditch effort the Egyptian Army has launched a
series of all-or-nothing strikes against the headquarters of the Nile River
Coalition (NRC) in Cairo.

Reports are coming in that after using ground troops to destroy NRC Directed
Energy Air Defense (DEAD) positions, the Egyptian Army mounted strikes
against the NRC Command and Control facilities in the Lotus Towers
mega-structure.

A source close to the Egyptian Army leadership has indicated that they
believe the NRC regional commander was killed in the raid. If this is the case,
this could well be a decisive moment in the battle for Cairo, at a time when
victory looked all but certain for the NRC.
