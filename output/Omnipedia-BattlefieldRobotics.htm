Battlefield Robotics

Of all of the various technological innovations of the last hundred
years, few have had as profound an effect on the battlefield as
robotics.

From the unsophisticated drones of the early 21st century and the

exoskeletons of the 2030's intended to enhance the warfighter, to
the bipedal

General-Purpose Infantry Units (GIU's) that entered wide-scale use
in the 2050's intended to replace them, battlefield robotics have
infiltrated every part of the modern war-space and revolutionized
our military playbooks.

Advances in Artificial Intelligence as well as revolutions in human
based control systems such as Direct Neural Interface (DNI) have
transformed the robotic participants in our conflicts from simple
human-proxies in hazardous situations into broadly autonomous
participants in their own right.

Conventional and Spec Ops forces are commonly supported on
the ground by a variety of robots:

• Autonomous Sentry Platform (ASP)

• Pilotable Autonomous Walking Weapons System (PAWWS)

• Hound, Orthrus & Cerberus ground assault systems.

• Guardian, Hardened Sentry and Power Core emplacements.

• General-purpose Infantry Units

As well as by close air support systems, where the deployment of
hostile Directed Energy Air Defenses (DEAD) allows:

• Talon escort drone systems.

• Wraith drones.
