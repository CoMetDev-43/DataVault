Pilotable/Autonomous Walking Weapons System (PAWWS)

Bipedal man-piloted heavy weapons platform, designed for urban
operations.

Following the military deployment of the fully autonomous General-purpose
Infantry Unit in 2048, a software upgrade gave the PAWWS the ability to
run without a human operator, or remote supervision.
