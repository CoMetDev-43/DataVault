Tacitus Corporation

The Tacitus Corporation was a military technology corporation
specializing in the research, development and manufacture of
drones and weapon systems.

After the death of Raul Menendez in 2025 and the subsequent
dissolution of his Cordis Die movement, US investigators
discovered that the Tacitus Corporation was in fact a shell
company owned by Menendez.

The corporation's assets were frozen, and trading suspended.

In 2028 the company's intellectual properties and patents were
sold off, with the Coalescence Corporation being one of the big
winners; a large number of their drone and robotic products are
based off of Tacitus Corporation products and research.
