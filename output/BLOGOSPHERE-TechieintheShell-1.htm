Techie in the Shell

10/21/59

As you know from my last post I have been selected to test the first round of
consumer DNI from Coalescence Corporation.

I have received all the paperwork to sign up, and it is a daunting amount of legal
mumbo-jumbo.

For the first time the realization hits me, this is a really big deal. The first consumer
testing of a Direct Neural Interface; my mind will be physically connected to a
computer, what that exactly means remains to be seen.

I would normally say what could go wrong, but it seems like there are very scary
answers to that question.
