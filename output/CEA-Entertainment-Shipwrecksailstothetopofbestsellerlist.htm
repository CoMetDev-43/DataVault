"SHIPWRECK" SAILS TO TOP OF THE BESTSELLER LIST

Shipwreck, the 23rd novel in the Don Summit series was released on
Tuesday and has already jumped to number one on the Current Events
Aggregate bestseller list.

Following up on the success of Fireball Dawn and Tidal Vortex, Ridley
Ransom's latest globe-trotting, action novella takes Don Summit on a
thrilling journey from the jungles of the Congo to the snowy slopes of
Switzerland as he battles the nefarious evil that is S.M.A.S.H.E.R corp.

Shipwreck - A Don Summit Adventure

"It is the greatest nautical technology in decades - the Proteus X1 - an
attack boat that transforms into a fighter jet and carries a nuclear payload.
There's only one problem: the Proteus X1 has disappeared and the only man
who knows where to find it has just been poisoned."

Shipwreck is available now on most e-reading and audio book platforms.

Publisher: YPMiller Press; 433 pages; English
