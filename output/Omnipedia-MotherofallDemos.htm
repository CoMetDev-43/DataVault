Mother of All Demos

The Mother of All Demos was a technology demo given by Douglas
Engelbart on December 9, 1968, at the Fall Joint Computer
Conference in San Francisco.

The demonstration featured a complete hardware and software
system call the oN-Line System (NLS).

This ninety minute presentation demonstrated main concepts
that would ultimately become integrated into future computer
systems including: hypertext, windows, graphics, video
conferencing, word processing, revision control and the mouse
input device.
