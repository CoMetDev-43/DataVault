Techie in the Shell

1/1/60

Happy New Year!

As you might be aware Zurich is cold in the winter, extremely cold. My wife
and I are spending the New Year at a ski lodge in Engelberg, we took the
Mag-Lev here from Zurich after our arrival last week.

This afternoon we will be heading back to Zurich, taking the train through
the snowcapped mountains and picturesque landscapes leading to
arguably the tech capital of the planet, where I will be prepped for a Direct
Neural Interface that will on paper allow me to directly connect my mind
with computers.

Basically, it's just your average day.
