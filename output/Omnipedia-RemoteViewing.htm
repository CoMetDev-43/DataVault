Remote Viewing

Remote viewing (RV) is the alleged ability to sense unseen targets
or objects using extrasensory perception. Generally, a person with
the ability to perform RV was expected to deliver information
about a hidden target objective from a great distance without the
use of the senses.

Remote viewing was introduced to the mainstream media in the
1990's when declassified documents were released about Project
Stargate, a $20 million covert research program operated by the
U.S. government.

Project Stargate was created by the Defense Intelligence Agency
to determine if remote viewing had any potential military
applications.

After several years of inconclusive evidence, erroneous data and
unreliable results, the program was dissolved in 1995.

The practice of remote viewing was never deemed useful in any
intelligence operation and there is currently no credible scientific
evidence that it works.
