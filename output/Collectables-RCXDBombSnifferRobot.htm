HCXD BOMB SNIFFER ROBOT

This HC-XD (Hovering Controlled eXplosive Device) bomb disposal
robot is based around a robot chassis currently being
manufactured in large quantities for military use by the Winslow
Accord and CDP.

The design is based around a now obsolete system (the RC-XD)
manufactured by the Tacitus Corporation in the mid-2020s, and
deployed by Special Forces.

The explosive charge has been vastly reduced in size, intended to
disrupt suspect packages and explosive devices, rather than inflict
wide-spread injury or death. The additional space left from the
reduction of the explosive payload has been filled with an array of
chemical sniffers, intended to inform the operator of proximity to
a wide variety of explosives.
