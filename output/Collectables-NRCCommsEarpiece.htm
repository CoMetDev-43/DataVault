NRC COMMS EARPIECE

A wireless comms headset used in an NRC Air traffic control
facility at the Abu Simbel airport.

The design is a fairly standard piece of equipment issued to CDP
command and control forces globally, and a clear step up from
equipment that the NRC had access to before aligning themselves
with the CDP.

Apart from some additional hardware used to provide end-to-end
broadcast encryption, there's little to distinguish the military
version of the headset from equipment designed for the civilian
marketplace.
