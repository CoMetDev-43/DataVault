SINGAPORE ARMED FORCES BATTLE CRIMINAL COMBINE

06/17/61 - 20:02 - SINGAPORE

Reports are coming in of gunfights in the streets of Singapore, where the
Singapore Armed Forces are attempting to intercept well-armed gangs of
criminals belonging to the infamous 54 Immortals (54I).

It is a little under two weeks since the Singapore Authorities voted to keep
the Quarantine Zone in place, despite evidence that toxicity levels inside of
the area may well be approaching 'safe levels'.

Local residents in the areas adjacent to the Quarantine Zone walls have
been reporting seeing large, well equipped and professional looking groups
of 54I going in and out of the quarantined area over the last week.

"They go in at night with trucks loaded with supplies", said one resident who
refused to be identified, "then come out 4 or 5 hours later, with all kinds of
stuff hidden under tarps."

"They're probably paying off some of the guards to let them through a check
point", he went on to postulate.

The 54 Immortals are a ruthless criminal organization, more militia than
gang, which has long been associated with the Singapore narcotics trade
as well as human trafficking.

Early indications from the monitoring of black-market channels suggest that
the 54I may be in the process of looting the premises and storage facilities
of the various tech companies that were based in the Quarantine Zone,
prior to the Coalescence Corporation Disaster.
