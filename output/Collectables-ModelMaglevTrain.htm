MODEL MAGLEV TRAIN

A 3D printed (scale model) of the Swiss Mag-Lev train encountered
in the Winslow Accord Cyber-Soldier program's training
Immersion.

The model floats over its base, 'levitated' by a pair of tiny rare-earth
magnets.

Analysis: Kane, R

The magnets are a cute touch. The real thing uses an
Electromagnetic Suspension system to float the entire train about
15mm off of its track. Doesn't sound like a lot, but that's a 62 ton
vehicle, floating and being pushed forwards at up to 500km/h.

Those are some strong magnets.
