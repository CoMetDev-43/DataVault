The Druon Leak

The Druon Leak was the 2037 release of a large quantity of
sensitive documents that had been stolen from the CDP's central
servers by the hacking group Druon.

It was significant as it proved that the CDP and the NRC were
working closely together, several years before the NRC formally
joined the CDP – tipping intelligence communities off to the CDP's
interest in Kenya's strategically important Rare-Earth element
resources.

Additionally, the leak revealed details of some of the CDP's covert
financial streams that the Winslow Accord were able to shut down.

The leak caused many to speculate that Druon were aligned with
the newly formed Winslow Accord, or one of her allies.

The group refuted this by releasing compromising photographs
and voice memos stolen from the United States Secretary of State
George Winslow's cell phone.
