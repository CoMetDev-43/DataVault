Propaganda

Propaganda is information that is used to influence the attitude or
belief of a person or population to further an agenda. Often

propaganda presents facts or information that is intentionally
misleading in nature.

Propaganda, although often associated with negative activities,
can also be used in positive ways such as encouraging public
awareness or education.

Types of propaganda:

White propaganda is information acknowledged by a source,
often times a government or institution and often reflects an
official viewpoint. This type of propaganda is generally true and
factual.

Gray propaganda is information that is deliberately ambiguous.
The information released may seem to emanate from a
non-official source, a government source, a potentially non-hostile
force or there may no source revealed or attributed at all.

Gray propaganda may in fact be questionable information whose
accuracy could be easily questioned.

Black propaganda is information that's reportedly generated by
one side, but in actuality created by the other side involved in a
conflict. The goal of the opposition is to use false information to
discredit, slander or vilify their adversary.
