Masud Assaf

Brother - 08.01.63

My dearest brother Hassan, I apologize but I have been unable to contact
you since the horrible bombing in Faqus.

We have been moved to a new camp and I could not send any
communication in transit. I hope this method will be suitable, I know mother
will miss the hand written letters but we are moving too fast for those now. I
am only able to send out these small messages once or twice a week if I am
lucky.

The new camp is filled with troops. The conditions here are tense; we drill for
hours, sleep and then drill again. It feels like all we do is wait for battle. I hope
it comes soon. I want to defeat these invaders and get back home.

Tell mother to keep praying for me.
