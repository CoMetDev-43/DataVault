COALESCENCE DISASTER - TWO YEARS ON

06/02/62 - 11:14 - SINGAPORE

Two years on from the horrific events of that morning in June that cost the
lives of over 300-000 men, women and children and there's still no sign of
any kind of return to normalcy for the area now known as the Singapore

Quarantine Zone.

Within weeks of the report that stated that death rates were trending
towards 'normal background levels', despite the continuing slow-die-off of
the unfortunates trapped within the cordoned off area, word began coming
in of the infiltration of forces loyal to the feared criminal combine, the 54
Immortals (54I) into the Quarantine Zone.

The Singapore Armed Forces were either unable or perhaps unwilling to offer
much resistance, and the 54I were able to operate freely, setting up forward
operating bases and mounting scavenging raids into the abandoned
buildings, laboratories and manufacturing facilities that make up much of
the area.

The Singapore Authorities requested aid with the defense of the Quarantine
Zone, but perhaps due to the vast sums already spent on aid for the
survivors there was little international will to help out, the message was loud
and clear: This is a regional issue, and should be dealt with by the elected
government of Singapore.

Now, grown incredibly wealthy having added the black-market sales of
classified technology and information from the ruins of the once-vibrant
tech hub to their portfolio of operations, already bloated with the sale of
illegal drugs and human trafficking, the 54I have established permanent and
well-defended bases of operations inside the Quarantine Zone, taking full
advantage of the water front they control to facilitate international trade.

Just 2 days ago, the Singapore Authorities acknowledged that they had in
fact lost control of the Quarantine Zone to the organization, warning them
to limit their activities to within the walled compound or face reprisals.

Quite what form those reprisals might take are anyone's guess. Based on
how ineffectual they were at preventing the takeover of the area, it should
come as no surprise that the 54I's response was derisory.

"Let them come", the leader of the 54I, Goh Min was quoted as saying,
"they'll be returned to headquarters piece by slowly flayed piece."

"The 54I will defend what it has rightfully taken", added his sister Goh Xiulan.
"We fear nothing."
