MANTICORE

The Manticore is an oversized heavy assault exoskeleton, manufactured

in low numbers and used exclusively by the CDP.

Heavily armed and armored, Manticores are capable of large=scale

devastation, however their size and relative low speed makes them

vulnerable targets.
