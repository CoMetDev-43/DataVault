FU DOG FIGURINE

This statuette represents a Fu Dog, or Chinese Guardian Lion.
These statues, both large and small have been commonly found
throughout Chinese culture since the Han Dynasty, dating back to
around 200 BC. They are associated with protection.

The 54 Immortals, the criminal combine who control the Singapore
Quarantine Zone use them as common iconography to celebrate
the birth year of Goh sibling's grandfather, who was once the ruler
of the ruthless organization.
