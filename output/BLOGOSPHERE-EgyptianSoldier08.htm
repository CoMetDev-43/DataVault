Masud Assaf

Sniper 01.06.63

Father, an enemy sniper reigned down hell on us today.

Six shots directly at our unit's position while we were smoking near the toll
booth.

Two hits. One man was wounded, the other... Yazid, my dear friend, was
slain.

Ottah, Naeem and I prayed over him.

We will avenge him.

It's a sad day. Pray for him. Pray for me.
