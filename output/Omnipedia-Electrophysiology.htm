Electrophysiology

Electrophysiology is the academic study of the electrical
properties of biological systems. The field concerns itself with the
actions (voltage or current changes over time) of single ion
channels in individual nerve cells, all the way up to the
macro-electrical properties of organs such as the heart.

As with the study of the processes in any living cell, the work
involved is incredibly exacting, often requiring nanometer perfect
positioning of microscopic pipettes and electrodes on particular
features on the surfaces of the cells being studied.
