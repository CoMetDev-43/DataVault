Operation Papillion

Operation Papillion was the codename for a clandestine
misinformation program created by the CIA during an
undisclosed period in the late 2020's.

The operation was apparently created in an effort to combat
continuous leaks of confidential information due to the
proliferation of anti-government hacker groups.

Known activities the operation was reported to have engaged in
were the following:

· White propaganda releases

· Gray propaganda releases

· Black propaganda releases

· Psychological warfare

· Targeted release of diversionary media
