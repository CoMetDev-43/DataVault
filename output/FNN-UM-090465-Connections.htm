NEW CONNECTIONS BETWEEN SPRING HEELED JACK AND

THE MOTHMAN

The similarities between the Spring Heeled Jack of London and the

Mothman of Point Pleasant are the core of the new book From Spring

Heeled Jack to The Mothman: The Many Faces of Terror, by Shepherd

Reagan. The book details the links and history of this fiendish creature,

from the Orang Bati of Indonesia and Tulpa of Tibet to the high

strangeness of Point Pleasant and the collapse of the Silver Bridge in

1966. What was most interesting was the author’s compilation of man

bat sightings leading up to the Coalescence Corporation Disaster,

and how the disaster was prophesized by a young man in Singapore

weeks before the event.
