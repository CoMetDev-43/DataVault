Immersion

Immersion is the name given to a form of Virtual Reality (VR),
experienced by people fitted with Direct Neural Interface (DNI)
systems.

Unlike conventional VR systems that are reliant on head mounted
or otherwise worn displays as well as other equipment to provide

haptic feedback, DNI systems allow simulated environments to be
experienced 'naturally' by all of a users' senses.

Additionally, a DNI system running in Immersion mode,
intercepts all of the users' movements; they traverse the
environment as if they were actually walking or running around it.

So far, Immersion systems have been limited to military
simulations that allow for intensive, risk-free hyper-realistic
training, as well as providing advanced interfaces for DNI
equipped individuals working in high-risk, high-focus
environments such as air traffic control, drone swarm controllers,
hypersonic pilots and surgeons.

The potential for the technology for entertainment is obvious and

startup companies are springing up rapidly, but the invasive and
expensive nature of the current generation of the Coalescence
Corporations' DNI technology means that while R&D costs remain
high, the potential market for consumer grade Immersion
products remains limited.

While DNI allows for the total Immersion of its users in simulated
realities, it can also be used to simply overlay data, or enhance the
content being experienced, allowing for the implementation of far
less all-enveloping Augmented Reality (AR) systems.
