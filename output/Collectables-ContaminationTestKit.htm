CONTAMINATION TEST KIT

This abandoned field contamination test kit was found in the
wreckage of a temporary lab tent outside of the abandoned

Coalescence Corporation facility in the Singapore Quarantine Zone.
In the aftermath of the Coalescence Corporation Disaster in 2060,
teams of medical experts were amongst the first allowed back into
the newly established quarantine zone.

In amongst the ongoing grisly work of identifying and disposing
of the tens of thousands of bodies, the search was under way to
isolate and understand the toxin or contaminant responsible.

No single cause was established; it was never even ascertained
whether the toxin was biological in nature.

As a result, the Quarantine Zone became a more permanent
feature.

Many of the vials and packages of the various reagents and
catalysts that the kit contained are missing.
