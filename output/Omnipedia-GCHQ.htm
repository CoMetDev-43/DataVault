GCHQ

The Government Communications Headquarters (GCHQ) is a
British intelligence security organization providing intelligence
and information to the British Government and the British armed
forces. Operating out of "The Doughnut" in Cheltenham it works
under the Joint Intelligence Committee (JIC) which presides over
the Secret Intelligence Service (MI6) and the Security Service
(MI5).

Originally established as the Government Code and Cypher
School after the First World War, it gained recognition for its role
in breaking the German Enigma Codes, and changed its name to
the Government Commination Headquarters in 1946.
