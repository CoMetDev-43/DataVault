GERMAN ARTILLERY SHELL CASING

A replica of an 8.8cm German PzGr. 39/43 APCBC-HE shell printed
after returning from the bizarre Immersion experienced with Sarah
Hall in Kebechet.

The standard armor piercing explosive shell made by the
Germans in World War 2, it was produced in massive quantities.

This one was fired either by a Tiger II tank or by a PaK 43 anti-tank
gun, which both used the same 88mm KwK 43 gun.

Unlike the Caseless Liquid Propellant based artillery introduced in
the mid 2050’s or mass driver based ordinance, artillery of this era
used shells that were essentially "up-scaled bullets"; a shell sits on
top of an explosive charge, held in a large brass or steel casing.
