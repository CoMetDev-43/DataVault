Directorate of Intelligence

The Directorate of Intelligence ( DI ) is one of the four major
components of the Central Intelligence Agency. Its primary
function is investigation, analysis and sourcing of key data related
to the international interests of the United States government.

The DI is responsible for the preparation of the Presidential Daily
Brief (or PDB), which is intended to provide the President of the
United States daily insight into the latest intelligence on sensitive
international situations that warrant attention or analysis.

The current organizational structure has the DI broken out into
four regional analytic groups, six groups focused on transnational
issues and two analytical support units.
