DATE: 21 March 1963

MEMORANDUM FOR: J.T. Markum III

SUBJECT: re: Berkeley Recruitment Mixer

Jack,

Did you really expect me to sign off on 75 gallons of primer for "a group
painting event" without an explanation? Why do these people want to paint
a school bus and what the hell do we need with twelve buffalo skin teepees?
It sounds like you're building a commune?

I don't know how this worked under Butch, but this shit isn't going to fly under
my watch. Accounting is already up my ass about the amount of money we
spent in Cuba.

Jeannie is setting up a meeting next week so we can discuss all of these
expenses.

Please do me a favor and come prepared. Have Ted bring supporting
materials so that I can wrap my head around spending $21,000 on a
"recruitment mixer."

- Felix
