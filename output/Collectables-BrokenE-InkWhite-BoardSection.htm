BROKEN E-INK WHITE-BOARD SECTION

A damaged section of an electronic-ink based display board, found
in one of the labs in the abandoned Coalescence Corporation
Singapore facility.

The passive nature of the technology means that it still holds the
last image that it was displaying when the board was shattered;
likely in the explosions that ripped through the building at the
start of the Coalescence Corporation Disaster.

Analysis: Kane, R

That molecule looks like it's an organophosphate, although the
damage to the board means I couldn't tell you which one.

Could be an insecticide. Could be a nerve agent. Could just be an
EP additive for a manufacturing process.

Based on what happened here, there's speculation that there must
have been some kind of potent war-gas released. This might
support that that theory.
