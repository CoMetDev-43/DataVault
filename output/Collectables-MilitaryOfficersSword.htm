MILITARY OFFICER'S SWORD

This sabre was once presented to an officer in the Egyptian Army
to commemorate their promotion at the end of military training.

Purely ceremonial in function, it has never carried an edge and
would only have ever been worn as part of a dress uniform, far
from an active combat zone.

After the failed attack on the Nile River neighbors in response to
their dam programs, the NRC followed the Egyptians back across
their borders and laid siege to Cairo. Nowhere was far from an
active combat zone anymore.

This was found in a makeshift hospital and mortuary in Ramses
station. It's likely that things didn't go so well for its original
owner.

Analysis: Kane, R

When all of this is over, we should try to locate the family of the
sword's owner and return it, if we can.
