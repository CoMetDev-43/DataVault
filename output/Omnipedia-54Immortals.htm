54 Immortals

The 54 Immortals are a heavily militarized criminal combine, who
are the de facto governing power inside the Singapore Quarantine
Zone.

Their name is often abbreviated to 'The 54', 'The Immortals' or
simply 'The 54I'.

Origins

The Immortals were formed in Singapore in 1894, under the
leadership of Goh Qiang (lit. Better Goh), a first generation
Singaporean of Chinese descent.

Their membership was originally drawn primarily from migrants
of Chinese, Indian and Malaysian origins.

Their name, like that of many of the Chinese secret societies in
Singapore draws on Chinese numerology; both 5 and 4 and
considered unlucky, 4 because of its Mandarin pronunciation 'si'
which is identical to the word meaning 'death'. 5 is similarly
shunned as it sounds the same as the word for 'not' or 'never' (wu),
and is generally thought of as negative.

Thus the gangs' name translates as literally the 'Never Dying
Immortals.'

The society prospered throughout the 20th century, making its
income from organized crime, human trafficking and through the
supply of illegal narcotics. Apart from a brief period in 2048, the
Immortals have been led by descendants of Goh Qiang.

Philosophy

Perhaps unsurprisingly for a group composed of migrants from
other cultures, the Immortals cling to the traditions and icons of
their home nations, in particular the Chinese traditions of their
ruling dynasty. They additionally venerate both their own
ancestors as well as the fallen members of the clan.

Members of the 54 Immortals undergo an initiation rite rumored
to involve the mixing of the initiates' blood with that of the other
members in attendance, toasting each other with a mixture of the
mingled blood and rice wine. They swear allegiance to the clan in
front of an altar featuring a painting of Goh Qiang, before making
an offering of the remaining drink.

Oaths of loyalty are then taken. From that point forward, the new
member is considered part of a family.

A Coup from Within

In 2046, the ruling Goh dynasty was briefly disrupted when Hyun
Jian bodyguard of the combine's leader Goh Cheung (lit. Good
luck Goh) murdered him, declaring himself head of the clan.

Goh Cheung's two young children, Goh Min (lit. Clever Goh, b.
2037) and his elder sister Goh Xiulan (lit. Elegant & Graceful Goh,
b. 2036) were left in the care of a feared clan enforcer Jae Xiang.

In 2048 Xiang would kill Hyun restoring the now 11 year old Goh
siblings to power, serving as their advisor through their teens.

By all accounts, the twins still ruthlessly lead the organization
today.

The Coalescence Corporation Disaster and Its Aftermath

In 2060 the Coalescence Corporation Disaster would leave 300,000
people dead and lead to the creation of the Singapore Quarantine
Zone, the cordoning of 30 square miles of land teaming with the
abandoned research facilities and corporate head-quarters of
numerous high tech corporations, along with the unfortunate
survivors of the disaster.

Due to uncertainties as to which contaminants were responsible
for the deaths, the Singapore authorities opted to keep the area
under quarantine long-term, despite evidence at the 1 year mark
that the worst was over.

The 54I were able to exploit corruption rife amongst the rank and
file for the Singapore Defense Forces to move into the Quarantine
Zone in 2061, where they amassed significant wealth looting and
selling on the black market whatever they could get their hands on
from the abandoned corporate facilities contained within.

In 2062 the Singapore Authorities admitted that they had
effectively lost control of the territory to the by-now heavily armed
criminal combine. International aid efforts to the area were
promptly suspended.

Iconography

The Immortals typically mark their territory with the animals
corresponding to the Chinese astrology birth years of their
founder Goh Qiang and their current leaders. Thus their facilities,
equipment and turf are often embellished with Fu Dog (Goh
Qiang) and Snake (Goh Min) symbols.

Military Strength

The 54I's numbers easily rival that of the Singapore Defense
Forces. They are well disciplined, well trained and well equipped
thanks to the vast wealth of their criminal empire.

They have been able to obtain the latest military robotic hardware
on the black market, and are known to have employed Private
Military Contractors to provide training to their soldiers.

Latest estimates are that they have at least 5000 troops at combat
readiness levels, as well as several battalions of General-Purpose
Infantry Units (GIUs).

It is also entirely possible that they possess experimental
pre-market versions of systems that were being developed by the
various companies that used to call Singapore home.

Political Allegiances

While the 54I appear to be a broadly independent entity, the Winslow
Accord designates them a terrorist organization.

There is mounting evidence that the CDP has supplied the 54I
with military hardware on a number of occasions.
