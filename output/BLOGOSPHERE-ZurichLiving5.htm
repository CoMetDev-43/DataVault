Shopping and Concert in Antwerp

Tara's dad told her that she was spending too much time at the spa and
country club the last two weeks so he decided to fly her to Antwerp on his
company jet!! And duh!! She couldn't go on a trip without me! #BESTIES

So when we arrived at the suite we noticed there was only one soaking tub
(in my wing of the suite). Tara was so furious, but her dad calmed us down
and yelled at the front desk clerk. We ended up switching rooms on the
penthouse level and got two big soaking tubs (one in her room and one in
mine).

After a day jammed with shopping... I picked up a nice scarf from Gabrielle
Tobias pop shop, an amazing set of luggage from Rolf Reinhardt Prestige (for
next month's trip to Tokyo) and a little gold dog carrier for Zizi
(#bestpuppyever) at Le Petit Chiot - Tara's dad told us he had a surprise!

When we got back to the suite, he gave us two golden envelopes and a
dozen roses each! We were so happy we just ripped through those
envelopes! BAM! He got us backstage passes to the Aegyo Generation
concert!

It was so amazing! They had a giant giraffe and the lead singer, Dagger
makes me swoon!

Traffic on the way back to the hotel was terrible. Tara's dad said there was
some kind of protest against the government or something.

All I saw were young people with signs and they started to rock our limo, so
Tara and I were a little scared.

I hope this never happens in Zurich. People seem to be much more behaved
there.
