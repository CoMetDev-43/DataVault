54I DATA-TABLET

A fairly standard civilian grade data tablet taken from the corpse
of a 54I looter, near the entrance to the main silo inside the
abandoned Singapore Coalescence Corporation facility.

There is a single file loaded onto the device, but it appears to be
encrypted.

Analysis: Kane, R

Those amateurs used an off-the-shelf encryption system, it only
took a couple of seconds to crack that sucker wide open.

It looks like the 54I weren't here randomly. They were contracted
by the CDP to investigate an alleged abandoned CIA project run
out of the basement of this facility. We don't know how the CDP
knew it, but they allude to there being a mole inside the CIA. I'll
run this up the ladder.

- decrypt follows -

The contract is go.

Head to the Coalescence Corporation facility, down into the deep
substructure. According to our friends in the CDP there's
something hidden in the lowest levels; a CIA Black Project - they
have someone on the inside who unearthed some old project
details. The CDP are willing to pay, and pay big for any data we
can pull from the central servers down there.

We're being paid in salvage. Bring back anything down there that
isn't bolted down.

Scratch that. Take laser cutters. If it looks like it might fetch a
decent price, bring it back.

- decrypt ends -
