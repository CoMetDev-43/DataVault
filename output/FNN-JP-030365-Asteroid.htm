The asteroid 2052 CD98 is predicted to pass close to the Earth on

April 21.

The potentially hazardous flyby of 2052 CD98 will be at a distance of

less than 0.05 AU, and its diameter is estimated to be between

400-800 meters.

Objects of this size are big enough to cause large scale destruction if

it were to impact on the surface of the Earth.

The Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) which manages the Near-Earth

Object Program for NASA declined to comment on 2052 CD98.
