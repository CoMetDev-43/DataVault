Directorate of Science and Technology

The Directorate of Science and Technology ( DS&T ) is one of four
major organizational components of the Central Intelligence
Agency. The main objectives of the Directorate of Science and
Technology are to develop research, create and manage advances
in technology that can be directly applied to the United States
intelligence apparatus and its military organizations.

The DS&T supports the CIA's intelligence gathering mission in
two key ways. It enhances the Agency's effectiveness via the
application of innovative technology and it serves as a counter to
any new technologies that its opposition might develop.

The DS&T has pursued various scientific innovations throughout
its history. From projects exploring new areas of interrogation
like project MKULTRA, to Acoustic Kitty ; an attempt to surgically
implant eavesdropping devices into cats.
