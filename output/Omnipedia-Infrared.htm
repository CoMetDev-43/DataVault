Infrared

Infrared (IR) is a form electromagnetic radiation that's
wavelengths are longer than visible light and is not visible to the
naked eye. The spectrum of infrared energy begins on the red
edge at 700 nano meters (Frequency 430 THz) to 1mm (300 GHz).

Night Vision

Infrared is used for night vision when there is insufficient visible
light to see. This is done with image intensification and active
illumination. Active illumination uses image intensification
technology and an active source of illumination in the near infrared
(NIR) or shortwave infrared (SWIR) band. Examples of such
technology includes low light cameras, and night vision
equipment.

Thermography

Infrared radiation can be used to determine the temperature of
an object without the need for physical contact. Infrared radiation
is emitted by all objects according to their temperature; this allows
imaging when no visible illumination or active infrared
illumination is present. The amount of radiation on object emits
directly corresponds to its temperature, this allows thermography
to visualize the variations in temperature.

Communications

Infrared can be used for the transmission of data in short range
communication. Remote controls and IrDA devices use light-emitting
diodes (LEDs) to emit infrared radiation in a focused beam. The
beam is modulated to encode data to the receiver. The receiver
utilizes a silicon photodiode to then convert the radiation into an
electric current.
