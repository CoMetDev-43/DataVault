HOUND

Initially produced as a platform for multi-role missions, the Hound series of
unmanned ground vehicles find wide use in militaries globally. They are
highly customizable, and may be fitted with a variety of weapons systems.

Originally remotely piloted, they have since been upgraded with advanced
control software, enabling autonomous operation.
