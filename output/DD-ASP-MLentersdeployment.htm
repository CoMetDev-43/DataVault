ASP-C AND ASP-ML ENTER DEPLOYMENT PHASE

Members of the Winslow Accord are set to take delivery of the new Autonomous
Sentry Platform (ASP) later this month.

The ASP's armor protection has been increased with the incorporation of advanced
dynamic protection system (explosive reactive armor - ERA); the defensive aid suite
(DAS) has been upgraded as well with a new area denial anti-explosive system
(ADAE).

The Coalescence sourced target acquisition system is now gen four and reportedly
has decreased time to target by 200ms over gen three.

In addition to the 125 mm armed main turret of the ASP, new IFV (Infantry Fighting
Vehicle) turrets have been added to the vehicle, boasting an impressive amount of
firepower with the dual 7.62 mm machine guns.

The ML variant is armed with land-attack missiles, and can be used against fortified
emplacements.

The first delivery of 2000 units will be landing in the United States and China by the
end of the month, with the remaining 8,000 being delivered over the next year.
