Titanium

Name: Titanium

Symbol: Ti

Atomic Number: 22

Category: transition metal

Discovered in Great Britain in 1791 by British mineralogist
William Gregor, Titanium is a very strong, light transition metal
highly prized for its resistance to corrosion.

By density it is the strongest metallic element, and is frequently
alloyed with iron and aluminum to produce strong, light alloys
used in aerospace, military and medical applications.
