Translated from Mandarin

I cannot take this anymore. For the sake of my city and my own sanity, I
MUST speak out. My city is under attack.

Disease. Famine. Cartels. All running rampant.

I'm angry and I can't stop thinking about how... I have failed... how WE have
failed our great city.

These cartel bastards walk all over our people, take what they want. They
execute innocent people in the streets. They have no honor. No sense of
mercy. They contribute nothing to society and only erode it... like parasites
feasting on the body of a dying host.

I want to defend my city, but they won't let me anymore.

I used to be a member of the police force. I was told not to worry about the
quarantine zone, and to let them have it... those filthy animals.

My former unit, now disbanded because we were "counterproductive to the
situation at hand."

I couldn't take it anymore and have retired. I'm too ashamed of what we
have let transpire. I feel like 25 years of service to my city was for nothing.

Now, as I sit here and contemplate my city's future... I can only think of
revenge.

These cartel bastards, they took my city, my pride and what was left of my
family after the disaster had already taken so much from me.

My lovely wife, Tan, murdered...

Brutally taken from me by Goh Xiulan ...that despicable woman...no words
can describe the evil that lay within her.

I am not going to standby any longer.

I've been talking to people just like me. People who have had their lives
stolen from them by the cartel, the disaster, the food shortages, the disease.

We may be smaller in size than the immortals, we may have fewer weapons
than they do... but we have an unquenchable thirst for revenge.

We are going to mobilize and walk into the zone ourselves. I don't care what
city officials say, I am not going to stand by and watch these atrocities
continue. We are going to do something about it... even at the cost of our
own lives.
