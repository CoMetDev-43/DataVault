Tantalum

Name: Tantalum

Symbol: Ta

Atomic Number: 73

Category: Transition metal

Discovered in Sweden in 1802 by chemist Anders Ekeberg, Tantalum
is a hard, rare, corrosion-resistant gray-blue transition metal
widely found as a minor component of various alloys.

Its inert, biocompatible nature makes it ideal for use in medical
implants and cybernetics.

It is often found in the same minerals as the rare earth element

Niobium, and shares many chemical properties with it.

It is often found in alloys used for components in jet engines and
missiles.

Tantalum is 15 times rarer than Gold.
