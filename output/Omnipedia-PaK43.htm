PaK 43

The Pak 43 (Panzerabwehrkanone 43) was a German 8.8 cm
anti-tank gun used during the Second World War.

It was manufactured by Krupp, and approximately 2000 were
built and deployed between 1943 and 1945.

The breach loading 6.4 meter long gun was capable of firing an 88
mm shell in a flat anti-tank trajectory to distances of over 1000
meters, although when firing on a more elevated trajectory the
weapon had an effective range of around 4 km, and could fire as
far as 16 km.

Well trained crews were expected to fire 7 rounds per minute.

A modified version of the Pak-43, the KwK 43 was used as the main
armament of the Tiger II heavy tank
