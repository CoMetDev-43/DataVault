KN Weapon Platform

The KN Weapon Platform is a series of evolutionary open source
weapon designs.

They are the first highly successful open sourced locally
manufactured weapons.

Introduced in 2042 the platform found success as it was highly
customizable and very reliable, created with cheap alloy printers.

The schematics for the weapon first found distribution on peer to
peer file sharing and quickly spread, following a disagreement
between disgruntled employees.

The cheap cost, speed of manufacture, common caliber and
reliability of the KN Weapon Platform made the weapon a top
choice across many areas of conflict through the 2050's.

The KN-44 spread very quickly among firearm enthusiasts and
soldiers alike. In 2062 the CDP formally adopted it as their
standard issue assault rifle.
