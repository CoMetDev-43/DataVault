Wraith

The Wraith is a versatile multi-role medium support drone, prized for
combining the long range, rapid strike capabilities of a super-sonic capable
interceptor, with stealth and active optical camo capable smart-coatings.
Able to carry a wide variety of loadouts, the Wraith is a one-size-fits-all
solution for any number of mission profiles.
