COALESCENCE DISASTER - ONE YEAR ON

06/02/61 - 08:01 - SINGAPORE

A year has passed since the explosion at the center of the Singapore
Quarantine Zone, in what was once the Singapore R&D facility of the

Coalescence Corporation ; one of the world's leading medical and robotics
suppliers.

What was once a bustling technological hub, now has the feeling of a
post-apocalyptic wasteland - a wasteland surrounded by 20 meter high
razor-wire topped concrete walls, punctuated by guard towers and
machine gun nests; the only traffic in or out the diminishing number of white
trucks driven by hazmat suited scientists and medics who are still toiling to
isolate base causes for the loss of life, now known to be around 80% of the
estimated 400,000 people thought trapped in the Quarantine Zone at its
creation.

According to a report published today by the scientific body established to
isolate the contaminant responsible for the deaths, while no single toxin has
been isolated that caused the horrific injuries seen in the first few weeks
following the accident, the prevailing death rate inside the walls is down to
only 25% higher than that outside of them. Despite this news, the authorities
have voted unanimously against rescinding the quarantine order, issuing a
statement that reads "While the news is encouraging, it is our paramount
duty to protect the health and wellbeing of all of our citizens. To that end,
until a categorical all clear has been issued by the relevant bodies, the
quarantine order must stay in place."

The closed hearings intended to establish culpability for the disaster ended
today. The findings are expected to be published in the coming days, but
early leaks indicate that no root cause of the explosion has been
established, none of the declared compounds in the building inventory
supplied by the Coalescence Corporation board could have caused the
injuries seen in the aftermath, and that no guilt of wrong doing or negligence
has been proven.

A full report is not expected to be made public, due to the confidential
information it would contain about the Coalescence Corporations
up-coming products.
