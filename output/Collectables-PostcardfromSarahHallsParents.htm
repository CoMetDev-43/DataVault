POSTCARD FROM SARAH HALL'S PARENTS

This is a postcard that you found amongst Sarah Hall's personal
possessions in her hideout aboard the huge Mobile Water Refinery
in the desert outside of Cairo, a vast derelict machine used in the
construction of the failed Sub-Cairo aquifer program.

It depicts a waterfall and trees in a public park in Singapore, and is
post-marked the day before the Coalescence Corporation Disaster.

Analysis: Kane, R

It seems that Hall's parents were vacationing in Singapore when
the Coalescence Corporation Disaster occurred. I can't find their
names on any of the survivor's lists.
