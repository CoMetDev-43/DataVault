DAMANGED OPTICAL CAMO CLOAK

Yet more state of the art CDP military technology in the hands of
the 54 Immortals.

This active camouflage system, intended to worn as an outer layer
over body armor is coated with a Smart Meta-Material layer that
actually causes the light falling on it to 'flow' around its surface,
emerging on the opposite side travelling in the same direction
that it started in, rather than bouncing back allowing the wearer to
be detected.

The operator could control the surface electronically, making
themselves selectively invisible in either the visible spectrum or at IR
wavelengths, rendering themselves invisible to heat based
targeting systems.

This one has been damaged, the control circuitry for the power
regulators having taken gunfire. As a result, the meta-materials
are altering their properties almost at random causing parts of the
cloak to fade in and out, making it useless as camouflage but
rendering it quite hypnotic to look at.
