RUSSIAN HAT

A replica of a Red Army Ushanka hat, made from sheepskin, and
lined with rabbit fur. Flaps designed to cover the ears to protect
them from frostbite in arctic conditions can be fastened under the
chin, or tied to the crown of the hat when not in use.

The rabbit fur lining indicates that this one was worn by an officer.

The Ushanka, or 'Trooper Hat' have been is use in Russia,
Germany and the Scandinavian countries for hundreds of years,
although the perfectly round version that everyone is so familiar
with is a 20th century invention.

Ushanka's were briefly replaced in the Red Army with a hat called
a 'budenka' made of felt, but it was abandoned when it was found
to offer inadequate protection from the elements.
