Directorate of Support

The Directorate of Support is one of four major organizational
components of the Central Intelligence Agency. It serves as the
administrative unit of the CIA and consists of security, logistics,
communications and information technology groups that support
the agency as a whole.

The Directorate of Support was created to provide logistical
support not only for operations run by the CIA during peacetime
but also during major conflicts. This includes providing a warzone
support base with communications, medical accommodations as
well as training and storage facilities.

This directorate also operates proprietary airlines for the CIA.
These aircraft provide covert logistical support in areas where
military aircraft might draw undue attention.

Two operations training facilities are also included under the
Directorate of Support's purview. These facilities are known as The
Point or Harvey Point and The Farm. Although unconfirmed, the
training received at these facilities incorporates a number of
disciplines including surveillance, cryptography and paramilitary
training.
