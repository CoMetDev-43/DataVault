Masud Assaf

Station 19.01.63

Family, it was so good to see you on the video call.

I am sorry about the roof and I wish I could be there to repair it for you.

I want to let you know that my unit will be moving again. I cannot tell you the
exact location, but I will continue to write here. Just know that I am going to
help others like you and to defend our land from the continued attacks.

Please be safe.
