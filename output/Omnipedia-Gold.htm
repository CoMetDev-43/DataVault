Gold

Name: Gold

Symbol: Au

Atomic Number: 79

Category: transition metal

Gold is a bright yellow, dense, soft and malleable metal highly
prized for both its appearance, as well as for its physical properties.

It is an excellent conductor of electricity and heat, and is highly
resistant to corrosion and the effects of most acids.

Atomically thin sheets of gold may be employed as shielding
against infrared light. Its excellent conductivity sees it widely used
in a number of electronics applications.

Heavier than iron, gold is thought to be produced when either
white dwarf or supergiant stars exhaust their fuel, and collapse
inwards before exploding in catastrophic supernovae explosions,
in which lighter elements are fused into heavier ones.
