DRAGON NECKLACE

Carved from Jade, this ornately crafted necklace depicts a Chinese
Dragon in flight; a potent symbol of wealth, courage and good
fortune.

It most likely was made to commemorate the birth of Goh Min,
former leader of the 54 Immortals, who was born in the Chinese
Year of the Snake, and given to his older sister Xuilan as a
keepsake of her baby brother.

Analysis: Kane, R
