Zombie Gas

Zombie Gas or Agent 15, is a nerve agent capable of paralyzing
victims placing them in a zombie-like state.

The gas, sometimes known as EA 3167, is an incapacitating agent
that induces long-lasting delirium and blocking nerve pathways
causing involuntary muscle movement.

Large doses of the agent can prove fatal.

Agent 15 has been linked to the CIA's covert chemical and
biological weapons program known as MKOFTEN.

Although unsubstantiated, the international intelligence
community believed that several nations outside of the United
States had access to and were producing large quantities of Agent
15 in the 1980's and 1990's.

These claims saw increased traction when a story circulating on
the internet claimed that Agent 15 may have contributed to
various illnesses encountered by veterans of the Gulf War in 1990.
