KOREAN POP BAND "AEGYO GENERATION" OPENS TO SELL OUT CROWDS

K-pop sensations Aegyo Generation opened to a massive crowd of over
400-000 in Seoul- South Korea this weekend to kick off their four leg
stadium sized Dae-bak tour consisting of over 210 shows and boasting the
world's largest (and likely first) neon animatronic zoo complete with 80ft
robotic crystal Giraffe.

If you haven't discovered Aegyo Generation's distinct sound yet, then you've
probably been living under a rock for that last two years as their sugary
sweet debut single "Bubblegum Bingo" took social media by storm racking
up over 452 million impressions in just over 16 weeks.

Featuring a rotating membership of six K-POP mega stars (Avalon Sae-Jun,
Teddy Hwa, Typhoon One, Jet Jin, "Butterfly" Hoon- along with K-Pop idol
slash action star Dagger Kang-Hwan) Aegyo Generation was formed by
South Korean billionaire Bae Park-Joon (also known by her stage name as
"Sunrise") in 2055.

The tour is expected to take at least three years to complete and will
culminate in the group's first concert movie, a 4D interactive experience
which Park-Joon says will "revolutionize the way concerts are watched in the
home."

Tickets for the 72 city, first leg of the Dae-bak tour go on sale Monday via
their website. Prices will range from $65 - $575 plus fees.

Update:
