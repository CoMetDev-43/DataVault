Masud Assaf

Checkpoint 19.03.63

Father, today was an incredibly hard day.

My unit moved to a very remote checkpoint on the outskirts of town.

Our commander seemed very nervous all day, I think this was due to the
number of hit and run attacks around the city.

Twelve men have been killed so far.

Please don't let mother worry.
