Henri Favager

Henri Pierre Favager was the French agriculturist attributed with
discovering evidence about the joint CIA and French clandestine
operation known as Project Udder Chaos.

Favager, a retired dairy farmer, purchased a one hundred acre
parcel of land near Saint-Fargeau, France where he accidentally
discovered a "buried locker box" filled with classified project
documents detailing parts of the CIA's clandestine effort to
"weaponize" various farm animals (Project Udder Chaos).

Almost immediately upon Favager's discovery of the documents,
the story went viral across international news and media outlets.

On May 26th, 2027, around three days after Favager's initial
discovery, the files were collected by the French government and
returned to the United States. Ultimately, the files and associated
documents were withheld from public view and sealed.

Around 2033, some of the alleged documentation found at the
Favager farm was leaked by the Antwerp based hacker collective
known as Druon in a massive data dump of 60,000 classified
documents.

Death

On December 21, 2037, Favager was killed in a single vehicle
collision in the French Alps.

The posthumous toxicology report filed by the local police stated
that Favager's blood alcohol level was 0.37%, well over the limit
that would induce a coma.

Since its publication in 2038, the police and subsequent coroner's
reports have fueled Internet speculation that Favager was the
victim of foul play.
