The Warsaw Purchase

The Warsaw Purchase was a treaty signed by Russia and Poland in
2025, in which Russia agreed to purchase Poland's debt following
the collapse of the European Union and the suspension of the Euro.
It would became the template for the treaties that would follow as
a new, east-facing trade block rose out of the ashes of the European
Union, guaranteeing preferential and protectionist trade policies
internally in return for energy security in the form of Russian
natural gas, and military hardware.

The text of the Warsaw Purchase remains more or less unchanged
in the articles of incorporation used by the CDP to this day.
