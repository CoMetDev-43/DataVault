United Nations

The United Nations (UN) is an intergovernmental organization
that was established 24 October 1945, to promote international
co-operation and prevent large scale conflicts such as the Second
World War.

At its inception the UN had 51 member states, there are now 198.
It replaced the League of Nations as the organizational body
promoting peace, human rights, and providing humanitarian aid
throughout the world.

The headquarters of the United Nations is located in Manhattan,
New York with offices in Geneva, Nairobi and Vienna.
