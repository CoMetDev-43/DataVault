Level I - Applied Nanoengineering

Day 1

My supervisor has asked that we create these blogs as part of the staff
wellness plan which is part of our overall performance review. To be honest,
I'm not someone who's overly self-indulgent or participates in social media,
but the HR folks were keen on new hires participating and also mentioned
something about the fact that "while we are a global research and tech
corporation, we do care for your well-being and that writing is often a
conduit to a healthy, more stable psyche."

I usually don't believe in that sort of hooey but I'm going to humor him (at
least until my probationary period is over - kidding!). HR mentioned that
everything written in these blogs is personal and confidential- but I'm taking
that with a grain of salt [sorry, I've been a skeptic all of my life.]

In all seriousness today is one of the most exciting days of my life. It's not too
often that a kid straight out of State gets plucked from relative obscurity (if
being Magna Cum Laude is obscure - I never said I was modest) and
placed into one of the most cutting edge divisions in one of the world's
foremost tech companies. It gives me goose bumps just writing it down.

Looking forward to this new chapter of my life.

Onwards and upwards.
