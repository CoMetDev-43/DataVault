DATE: 09/22/1962

TO: McCone, John

FROM: J. Kennedy

SUBJECT: Soviet Missiles

John,

Thank you for your report on the issue in Cuba. I will order an investigation
and take all appropriate action. Be prepared for action items in the next few
days.

And say hello to the wife and kids for me.

-Lancer
