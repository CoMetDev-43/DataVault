AM Band

AM Band (AM) refers to the broadcast frequency allocation
between 1611-1701 kHz, on which AM (amplitude modulation)
radio broadcasting takes place.

Amplitude Modulation was the first method by which sound was
successfully broadcast using radio waves.

Developed in the early 20th century, AM works by varying the
amplitude (or signal strength) of a fixed frequency carrier wave in
response to the signal being broadcast.

At time of writing, AM is only principally used by emergency
beacons and has been phased out in favor of more modern
techniques.
