BIO-LUMINESCENT ORCHID

The bio-luminescent orchid represents one of the first mass
market consumer products based on bio-engineering.

A Moth Orchid ( Phalaenopsis ) had the genes that code for the
production of Green Fluorescent Protein (GFP) commonly found
in the Crystal Jelly ( Aequorea Victoria ) spliced into its genome.

The gene expressed itself around the edges of the resulting plants
petals, creating striking displays in the dark.

Due to their limited availability and extraordinary novelty the
plants were incredibly popular when they were introduced, and
commanded high prices changing hands for thousands of dollars.

While the fad wore of quickly, many people still own these as
symbols of aspirational wealth.
