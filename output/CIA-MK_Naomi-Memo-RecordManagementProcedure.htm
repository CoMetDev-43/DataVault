DATE: 24 April 1957

MEMORANDUM FOR: OSI Division Branch Chiefs

SUBJECT: MK NAOMI - Record Management Procedure

Per D01947 - outlined below are the final and approved record
management procedures.

- Effective immediately - Lab(s) 001-013 will purge detailed logs of project
activities. - 24 April

- Local project logs will be placed into the retired record evaluation process
effective - 26 April

- OSI Branch Chiefs to tag all pertinent project documentation with
designators sent in accompanying packet {A, R, D} by no later than 29 April

- Records designated D to be sent to records center no later than - 01 May

- Records designated R to be sent to records center no later than - 10 May

- Records designated A to be sent to central archive site B2 no later than -
17 May
