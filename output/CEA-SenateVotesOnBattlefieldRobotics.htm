Senate Votes on Battlefield Robotics

06/15/42 - 07:20 - WASHINGTON, D.C.

After nearly two months of considerable debate the United States Senate
decided Tuesday that it simply does not want robots on the battlefield.

The Senate voted overwhelmingly - 73 to 25 against to the sweeping
battlefield robotics bill, squashing a measure that would have seen the first

General Purpose Infantry Units deploying as early as 2044.

"The concept is interesting, but we just don't think it's quite ready for prime
time," Sen. Shane McComb VII said shortly after the vote.

This marks the third consecutive vote against the modified BR bill in the last
four years.
