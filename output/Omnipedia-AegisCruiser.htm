Aegis Cruiser

The Aegis Combat System (ACS) is an integrated naval weapons
system that uses radar technology to track and guide weapons to
destroy enemy targets.

The Arleigh Burke -class of guided missile destroyers were the
United States Navy's first class of destroyer built around the Aegis
Combat System and the SPY-1D multi-function phased array
radar.

The word "Aegis" is a reference to Greek mythology; the Aegis was
the shield of Athena.
