LCAR-9USA

The LCAR-9 is a super-compact machine pistol designed as a
lightweight personal defense weapon.

While never intended to be used extensively in combat, the LCAR-9
offers acceptable performance and stopping power at close range,
making it a favorite backup weapon for military and police forces
requiring more firepower than a conventional semi-automatic
pistol.

The LCAR-9's size, portability, and ease of concealment have made
it popular with gunfighters all across the spectrum; from military
vehicle crews to private security groups to special forces units.

Although the LCAR-9 only supports a limited range of attachments
and modifications, it has become infamous for its extremely low
audible signature when using an attached silencer.
