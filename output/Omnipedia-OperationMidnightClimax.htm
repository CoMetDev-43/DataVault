Operation Midnight Climax

Operation Midnight Climax is the codename for a covert
sub-project of MKULTRA, the CIA ' s alleged mind control research
program of the 1950's and 1960's.

Operation Midnight Climax involved a network of CIA funded
brothels in cities around the United States. The goal of the
operation was to administer a wide range of substances to
unsuspecting victims via prostitutes on the CIA payroll.

Victims would be lured back to the brothels where they were
provided LSD or other drugs. CIA researchers would then monitor
these unsuspecting victims in specially designed rooms outfitted
with one-way glass.

The operation gathered a substantial amount of research in the
fields of blackmail, surveillance and the use of mind-altering drugs
in the field.

In 1962, an internal CIA investigation and subsequent report
condemned the project, which resulted in a significant reduction
in the program's size.

In 1966, the program was officially shut down.
