COALESCENCE DISASTER - ONE MONTH LATER

07/02/60 - SINGAPORE

In moving scenes commemorating the one month anniversary of the terrible
events that have so far cost the lives of an estimated 200,000 of their fellow
citizens, residents of Singapore took to the streets this evening to hold a
candle-light vigil.

They are demanding answers from their government and from the board of
directors of the Coalescence Corporation as to what caused the accident
one month previously; answers that no one seems any closer to be being
able to answer.

Each morning the official death toll is updated and new lists of the identified
dead are published, but for so many here the fates of their friends and loved
ones remain unclear.

What is certain is that that staggering number of casualties is set to rise.

Video feeds smuggled out of the Quarantine Zone show desperately unwell
survivors queued outside temporary medical facilities, resembling as much
as anything, the walking wounded pictured following the terrible gas-attacks
of World War 1.

Sources close to the Hazmat-suited scientific teams operating inside the
cordoned area indicate that at this time there is little they can do for the
sickest of the survivors beyond offering palliative care.

"We're dealing with numerous symptoms", said one, "but the damage that
these people have sustained to their skin and lungs is horrific. I've never seen
anything like it."
