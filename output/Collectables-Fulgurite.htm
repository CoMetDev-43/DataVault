FULGURITE

This branching, dendritic sculpture is a 3D printed representation
of a fulgurite that you found at the site of lightning strikes whilst
in an Immersion representing an interpretation of Cairo and its
surroundings.

You wouldn't expect to find something like this in a simulation.
Lightning strikes and other weather effects are normally purely
cosmetic; the attention to detail is extreme.

Fulgurites are formed when lightning strikes sand, fusing the
silica into a hollow glass tube; sometimes, when conditions are
just right, the whole branching pattern of the electricity
dispersing into the Earth is captured, resembling the root system
of a giant tree.

Some have been dug up that have been over 10 meters in length.
