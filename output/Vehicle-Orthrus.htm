ORTHRUS

The Orthrus is a variation on the Coalescence Corporation's hugely
successful Hound battlefield robotic platform. It is fitted with an additional
front-facing blast shield, enhancing its battlefield survivability.

The additional weight further limit's the platforms stamina on deployment.
