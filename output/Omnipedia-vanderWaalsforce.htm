Van der Waals Force

Named for the Dutch scientist Johannes Diderik van der Waals
(b.1837 - d.1923), the van der Waals force is a weak force of
attraction between electrically neutral molecules that pass very
close to each other.

They are much weaker than chemical bonds.

The van der Waals force allows water vapor to condense forming
liquid water.

In nature, the gecko's ability to climb on sheer glass surfaces is due
to van der Waals forces between the surface and the microscopic
features of the tiny hair like structures between their toes.

A number of climbing devices mimicking these features using

meta-materials have been trialed by the military.
