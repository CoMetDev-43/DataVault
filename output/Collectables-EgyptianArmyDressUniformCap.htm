EGYPTIAN ARMY DRESS UNIFORM CAP

This is a battered and worn Egyptian Army cap that once belonged
to Lt. Zeyad Khalil, who you met while mounting the mission to
rescue Minister Said from the hands of the NRC at Abu Simbel
airport in Ethiopia.

Its style is a little archaic to your eyes; it looks to be about 20 years
out of date. Its threadbare condition suggests that it may well be a
hand-me-down previously owned by numerous Egyptian soldiers.

With their resources strained by climate change driven
over-population, and their on-going war with the CDP allied NRC
over Nile water rights, the Egyptian army has understandably not
made it a priority to update their uniforms, and has channeled the
aid received from the Winslow Accord into supplies, weapons and
ammunition.
