BIO-CONTAINMENT CANISTER

A large capacity portable bio-containment canister, found in the
abandoned Coalescence Corporation Singapore Facility.

These canisters are typically used to hold hazardous biological
samples, such as live pathogens or virulent toxins.

Despite its age, the triple redundant hermetic seal systems appear
to be functioning.

Analysis: Kane, R

Unsurprisingly I'm getting negative power readings off of that
canister, which means I can't connect to the inventory system to
determine what that thing is holding.

Those things are manufactured to survive substantial impacts,
and are coated in the same self-repairing smart materials that
make up your body armor.

Lucky for us the containment is designed to remain active in the
event of power loss.

Be careful with that.
