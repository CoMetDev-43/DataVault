Mutual Defense Treaty

A Mutual Defense Treaty is an agreement between nations or
states, by which the signatories pledge to come to each other's
defense, or to offer each other support, when the conditions laid
out in the treaty are met.

Membership of Mutual Defense Treaties may shrink or grow over
time.

Depending on the wording of the treaty they may be used for
defense, or may be cynically interpretable for more aggressive use.

Often non-defense related agenda are also covered by Mutual
Defense Treaties, such as trade and population movement
between member states.
