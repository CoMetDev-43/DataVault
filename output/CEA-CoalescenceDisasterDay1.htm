COALESCENCE DISASTER - DAY 1

06/02/60 - 23:00 - SINGAPORE

Singapore is in a state of shock today after an explosion and subsequent fire
at a research facility belonging to the Coalescence Corporation, a
prominent defense supply company, has released unknown contaminants
into the atmosphere leaving many thousands of people dead.

Additional fires have been caused by emergency service VTOLs that
crashed while flying towards the stricken building, their crews apparently
falling victim to the toxin cloud themselves. At this time those fires are still
burning out of control.

A state of emergency has been declared by the Singapore Armed Forces
who have released aerial drone footage showing countless people lying
motionless in the streets for many city blocks around the site of the accident.

Singapore has formally requested international assistance; a Winslow Accord
relief force is setting sail to help with the investigation, clean up and massive
humanitarian aid effort required to assist the approximately 400,000 people
now trapped in a "quarantine zone" set up to contain the contamination.

Thoughts and Prayers

Coalescence Corporation officials have issued a single public statement;

"At this difficult and uncertain time, our thoughts and prayers are with the
families and friends of those who work at our Singapore Research Centre."

They have apparently promised full co-operation with the Civilian Authorities.

Too much to bear

Singapore Armed Force troops have been manning barricades set up on all
major thoroughfares into and out of the quarantine zone. Shots have been
fired to disperse crowds of refugees seeking to flee the area. Many of those
refugees appear in poor health, many apparently suffering from what look
like severe chemical burns.

Throughout the day hazmat suited investigative teams have been making
their way into the area, setting up forward bases of operations. One
investigator, speaking on condition of anonymity said "We were supposed to
be gathering samples from near the site of the explosion, but instead we've
been gathering bodies into make-shift morgues all day. I know what's being
officially said about the number of the dead at the moment, but the final
number may well be more than any of us can bear."
